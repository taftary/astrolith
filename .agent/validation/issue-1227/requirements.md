# Requirements (CONFIRMED) - issue #1227

Source: Spec v2, https://github.com/taftary/astrolith/issues/1227#issuecomment-6689687645.
Owner confirmation: https://github.com/taftary/astrolith/issues/1227#issuecomment-6689707126.

Runtime criteria below are judged from independent runs of the documented
public interface and their retained text evidence. Unit tests alone do not
prove these capabilities. Do not modify scenario files to make a result pass.

- [ ] C1: A documented, opt-in test interface launches the real application with isolated settings and a fixed universe seed. It identifies the executable and scenario, preserves the real player settings, and ends with a clear success or failure result. (judged by the validator from its own scenario_run.py commands, manifest.json, stdout.txt, stderr.txt and app/scenario.tsv; no script probe)
- [ ] C2: Reproducible scenarios can prepare and report planet-surface, near-moon hovering, within-system space, between-system space, and between-galaxy space starting states. The near-moon state places the real player in flying mode just above an actual moon and reports it as hovering, not grounded or landed. Preparation is recorded separately from actions under test. Fixtures include different galaxies, a planet with a moon, and a destination unvisited at scenario start. (judged by the validator from independent planet, planet-other, near-moon, system, interstellar and intergalactic runs and a repeated starting state; no script probe)
- [ ] C3: The validator can drive clicks, pointer movement, scrolling, and key press/release through the same input handling used by the player. Demonstrate this on existing menu navigation and existing movement controls. Tests must not call internal gameplay actions or directly rewrite player position to perform the behavior being tested. (judged by the validator from independent planet, menu and scroll runs and their action traces and observations; no script probe)
- [ ] C4: Read-only text evidence reports actual player identity and position separately from camera position; containing galaxy/system/body where applicable; and surface clearance where applicable. It supports before/after comparisons with clear units and coordinate context. For the near-moon state, identify the moon and flying state; any reported separation from its rendered surface must be labeled as geometric separation, not proof of collision ground or landing. It reports whether a fixture destination was visited in the scenario, without inventing a new player-facing discovery system. (judged by the validator from independent planet movement and camera-only observations, near-moon and second-galaxy evidence; no script probe)
- [ ] C5: Scenarios can check expected observations, wait for observable readiness with bounded timeouts, and fail clearly for missing controls, unmet assertions, invalid input, or timeout. They retain action traces and application logs on failure, and do not report an incomplete run as successful. (judged by the validator from independent missing-control, unmet, malformed, timeout and outer-timeout runs, retained evidence and existing-output refusal; no script probe)
- [ ] C6: The testing interface supports observing actual UI selection and control state, so the future mini map can expose its browsing location, selected destination, and confirmation availability without the test driver supplying fabricated values. Prove this observation mechanism on existing controls. Future map-specific observations and feature tests remain part of #1226. (judged by the validator from independent menu and scroll evidence of actual panel selection, Interaction and a changed setting, plus documented feature-specific availability limits; no script probe)
- [ ] C7: An independent validator can use the documented interface to reach a planet-surface starting state, operate existing UI, move the player, distinguish movement from camera-only changes, and exercise a negative case. Record exactly which capabilities were proven and any remaining limits in docs/validator-capabilities.md, explicitly excluding unproven moon landing or moon ground collision. (judged by the validator from its own public-interface runs and the capability registry, without editing repository code; no script probe)

No teleport, mini map, persistent discovery or moon landing claim is permitted.
Settings/Help have no player-facing change. Main-agent and independent evidence
must be distinguished. The owner tests product acceptance after merge.
