# Requirements — Issue #1085

Spec v1: https://github.com/taftary/astrolith/issues/1085#issuecomment-6473924314
Owner approval: https://github.com/taftary/astrolith/issues/1085#issuecomment-6473940761

- [ ] C1: Reproducibility: exact baseline/prototype commits, environment, fixed configurations, scenes, commands, raw evidence, and limitations are recorded so another tester can repeat the comparison. (judged by the validator from README.md and the t2-t6 evidence folders; no script probe)
- [ ] C2: Diagnosis: separate CPU, GPU where available, and wait/frame-delivery evidence supports a ranked bottleneck assessment. All listed rendering/loading areas are assessed or explicitly marked unmeasurable with a reason; unsupported causal conclusions are not presented as findings. (judged by the validator from t1/README.md and t2/README.md; no script probe)
- [ ] C3: Bounded prototype: one representative candidate addresses a measured cost, can be selected explicitly for testing, and leaves ordinary startup on baseline rendering. The reason for selecting it and technical mobile risks/unknowns are documented. (judged by the validator from t3/README.md, t5/REPORT.md and a normal launch; no script probe)
- [ ] C4: Measured verdict: short-run and sustained evidence follows the protocol. The report classifies the candidate as promising, rejected, or inconclusive against the numerical, visual, and resource rules, including all failing scenes. Failure to improve is an acceptable research outcome, not a successful optimization claim. (judged by the validator from t4/README.md and t4/t4-pairs.json; no script probe)
- [ ] C5: Visual and behavior coverage: paired images and motion evidence cover the listed scenes and transitions. The report checks retained worlds, lighting/night/exposure, marker readability, first-person surfaces, ground support, navigation, and atmosphere continuity. Existing correctness checks retain their thresholds. Any failing prototype behavior is disclosed and blocks a rollout recommendation. (judged by the validator from t5/README.md and the paired pictures; no script probe)
- [ ] C6: Controls and default experience: existing settings/help remain accurate; normal launch, Auto/Manual, saving/restarting, and Reset retain baseline behavior. Prototype activation cannot persist accidentally as a saved player preference. (judged by the validator from t5/real-window/ and the prototype tests; no script probe)
- [ ] C7: Owner decision package: provide a concise recommendation explaining the cost, visible trade-offs, resource/loading effects, target gap, and next action, with an executable sample and repeat instructions. Wider rollout remains subject to owner visual confirmation and a separately approved scope. (judged by the validator from t5/REPORT.md and README.md; no script probe)
- [ ] C8: Honest platform coverage: desktop findings and mobile design considerations are clearly separated. Real-device Android/iOS/iPadOS compatibility, memory, thermals, and sustained performance remain unvalidated unless separately evidenced through #1054. (judged by the validator from t5/REPORT.md; no script probe)
- [ ] C9: Launch normally with disposable settings. Expect the baseline look and familiar controls, with no new graphics choice or changed default. (judged by the validator from a disposable normal launch and t5/real-window/; no script probe)
- [ ] C10: View baseline and prototype in daylight ground, first person, orbit, star system, and a second world. Expect matched views that make visual trade-offs apparent, with readable markers and recognizable objects. (judged by the validator from t5/pictures-base/ and t5/pictures-inflight1/; no script probe)
- [ ] C11: View moonlit/moonless ground, dawn/dusk, rapid turns, LOD transitions, and continuous descent. Expect preservation of required behavior; any failure must be explicitly demonstrated and classified as a reason not to roll out that candidate. (judged by the validator from t5 pictures and t4 frame data; no script probe)
- [ ] C12: Review the measurements and sustained route. Expect the report to identify what became cheaper, whether frame delivery improved, which scenes regressed, and what remains unknown. A negative or inconclusive result must be described plainly. (judged by the validator from t4/README.md, t6/README.md and t5/REPORT.md; no script probe)
- [ ] C13: Change existing quality/exposure controls, restart normally, and use Reset with disposable settings. Expect baseline persistence/reset behavior and no accidentally retained prototype selection. (judged by the validator from t5/real-window/; no script probe)
- [ ] C14: Review the recommendation and sample. Expect mobile claims to remain limited to design considerations without device evidence. Accepting this investigation does not itself authorize rollout. (judged by the validator from t5/REPORT.md and README.md; no script probe)
