# 0011 — L2/L3 merge with renumbering

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-04
- Issue: #151 (spec v3 approved; owner decisions Q1, Q6/Q6b on record)

## Context

Old L2 (cosmic web, 1.37 Gly) and old L3 (superclusters, Laniakea) sat
0.42 decades apart, so a cosmic-web cell held five superclusters. The wall
is a length and Laniakea a diameter; nesting one inside the other as
parent and child rung was never physical.

## Decision

Old L3 folds into L2 as content: L2 is the cosmic web containing
superclusters (anchor stays the Sloan Great Wall; Laniakea lives inside
as the home supercluster). Old L4–L11 shift down one number; Earth lands
at L10; the beyond-MVP tail becomes L11–L14. The ladder table, labels,
exponents, journey (now L1–L10 through the Local Group), and goldens move
together. Gaps over 1.5 decades are crossed through invisible
magnification milestones that carry no frame state.

## Consequences

`MAX_LEVEL` is 14, `MAX_OPEN_LEVEL`/`MAX_NAV_LEVEL` are 10. Follow-up
Issues #152–#157 still say L1–L11 in carried-over text; each reconfirms
its level references at its own clarification.
