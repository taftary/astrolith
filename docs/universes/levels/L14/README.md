# L14 — Room

L14 = L14 (ladder row L14, Room, 10^0–10^1 m). This
folder is the L14 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #231): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L14 ends the beyond-MVP tail at the 1 m end point: many rooms per building, then nothing below.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l13.md` — L13 behind us: what the buildings with houses and blocks
  look like, what carries over into L14, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L13-to-L14 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `shell.md` — the enclosure: floors, walls, ceilings, doors,
  windows and glazing.
- `furniture.md` — the use: beds, sofas, chairs, tables,
  clearances and comfort rules.
- `storage.md` — the keeping: shelves, cabinets, closets, counters,
  reach and the work triangle.
- `light-power.md` — the served: lamps, fixtures, outlets, vents,
  radiators and wiring.
- `textiles-decor.md` — the soft: rugs, curtains, art,
  small objects and placement.
- `layout.md` — the plan: how the pieces sit in 1–10 m,
  circulation, the 1 m end point.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: single-room huts
  and ancient rooms through medieval halls, early modern specialization,
  industrial furniture and light, modern fitted rooms to today's rooms,
  plus the far-future fate under growth, material change and abandonment,
  with a reading guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a cross-check of
  aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`assembly-room-independence-nps`,
`destiny-lab-interior-nasa`), part close-ups (Fallingwater shell, Gropius
seating, Painted Desert cabinets, skylight lamp, Gresham parlor, Smithsonian
parlor plan), timeline endpoint (Monticello bedroom).
