# L02 — Cosmic web

L02 = L2 (ladder row L2, Cosmic web, 10^24–10^26 m). This folder is the L2
visual and lifetime reference (Issue #219): what its parts look like, how
they were created, how they changed through time, and how they interact —
each page with target-visual images and named sources. The bridge from the
previous level and the entry transition come first; the dated timeline runs
through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l1.md` — L1 behind us: what the Observable universe looks like,
  what carries over into L2, what changes at this zoom, key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L1-to-L2 dive in plain steps: approach, effects and
  visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `filaments.md` — threads and bridges: WHIM highways, finder methods,
  feeding nodes, Sloan/Quipu/BOSS/Coma examples.
- `walls.md` — sheets and pancakes: edge-on vs face-on views, DTFE look,
  CfA2/Sloan/South Pole walls, draining into filaments.
- `voids.md` — giant voids: Local push, Bootes map, KBC and Eridanus
  extremes, growth and merging, dark-energy probes.
- `superclusters.md` — basins of attraction: Laniakea home slice and flows,
  Shapley pull, Coma assembly, Quipu/Hyperion extremes.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: inflation seeds to present
  depletion plus far-future fate, with L2 numbers at every stage.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and license in
its doc. Bridge views (`earths-location-8maps`, `observable-universe-log`,
`2mass-lss-aitoff`), part close-ups (filament emission, DTFE Sloan wall,
Bootes map, local-supercluster map, Laniakea slice).
