# L03 — Galaxy clusters and groups

L03 = L3 (ladder row L3, Galaxy clusters and groups, ~10^23 m). This
folder is the L3 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #220): what its
parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through time,
and how they interact — each page with target-visual images and named
sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry transition
come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l2.md` — L2 behind us: what the Cosmic web looks like, what
  carries over into L3, what changes at this zoom, key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L2-to-L3 dive in plain steps: approach, effects and
  visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `clusters.md` — rich clusters: Coma archetype, Bullet and El Gordo
  mergers, hierarchical assembly, weighing methods, Virgo home.
- `groups.md` — poor groups: Local Group home, Stephan's Quintet shocks,
  fossil end-states, pre-processing before clusters.
- `icm.md` — intracluster medium: X-ray sea, early enrichment, cool-core
  feedback, SZ mapping, Virgo mass split.
- `members.md` — member galaxies: morphology sorting, brightest cluster
  giants and M87's jet, jellyfish stripping, orphan-light tracers.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: seeds to first groups to
  protoclusters to El Gordo to today (13.787 Gyr) plus freeze-out fate,
  with L3 numbers at every stage and part cross-checks.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and license in
its doc. Bridge views (`virgo-wide-eso0919c`, `abell1689-hst`,
`perseus-chandra`), part close-ups (Bullet composite, Stephan's Quintet,
M87 jet, Planck expansion history).
