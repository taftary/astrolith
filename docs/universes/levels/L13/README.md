# L13 — Buildings

L13 = L13 (ladder row L13, Buildings, 10^1–10^2 m). This
folder is the L13 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #230): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L13 continues the beyond-MVP tail: many buildings per city, then rooms below.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l12.md` — L12 behind us: what the cities with cores and grids
  look like, what carries over into L13, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L12-to-L13 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `houses.md` — the rows: houses, residential buildings, yards,
  lots and subdivisions.
- `blocks.md` — the walls: apartment and office blocks, mid- and high-rise,
  curtain walls and conversions.
- `commercial.md` — the glass: shops, offices, commercial cores,
  malls and big boxes.
- `civic.md` — the shared: schools, hospitals, civic buildings,
  campuses and precincts.
- `industrial.md` — the made: warehouses, factories, sheds,
  docks and chimneys.
- `stadiums.md` — the crowds: stadiums, arenas, large-span structures,
  bowls and roofs.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: shelters and first homes
  over thousands of years through ancient masonry and medieval timber,
  industrial brick and steel, concrete and glass to today's buildings,
  plus the far-future fate under growth, climate change and abandonment,
  with a reading guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a cross-check of
  aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`reliant-park-houston-nasa`,
`brasilia-stadium-nasa`), part close-ups (Barefoot Bay suburb, Manhattan
blocks, Chicago night core, DC avenues, ship-channel works, Levi's
stadium), timeline endpoint (Las Vegas night grid).
