# L07 — Outer solar system

L07 = L7 (ladder row L7, Outer solar system, 10^15–10^16 m). This
folder is the L7 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #224): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l6.md` — L6 behind us: what the Stellar neighborhood
  looks like, what carries over into L7, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L6-to-L7 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `giants.md` — giant-planets zone: Jupiter-to-Neptune layout,
  rings, large moons as distant points, colors and storms.
- `kuiper.md` — Kuiper Belt: classical and resonant populations,
  Pluto system, Arrokoth contact binary.
- `scattered.md` — scattered disc and detached objects: Eris,
  Sedna, sednoids, Planet Nine search.
- `oort.md` — Oort cloud shells: inner Hills cloud plus outer
  spherical reservoir, long-period comet source.
- `comets.md` — comets, Centaurs, interstellar visitors, plus the
  heliosphere boundary with the Voyager crossings.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: solar-nebula collapse
  over millions of years to giant-planet formation and migration,
  Kuiper sculpting and Oort emplacement, bombardment and comet
  showers, stellar passages and the Voyager era, plus the red-giant
  and far-future fate, with a reading guide mapping each stage to
  its part doc and a cross-check of aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`alpha-centauri-wide-eso1629i`,
`oort-cloud-nasa`), part close-ups (Neptune full disk, Pluto heart,
Eris and Dysnomia concept, Kuiper–Oort diagram, comet 67P),
timeline endpoint (Pale Blue Dot).
