# L10 — Planets and moons

L10 = L10 (ladder row L10, Planets and moons, 10^6–10^8 m). This
folder is the L10 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #227): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L10 is terminal: one planet with its moons, the journey's end.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l9.md` — L9 behind us: what the star close-up
  looks like, what carries over into L10, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L9-to-L10 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `interior.md` — the engine: core, mantle, crust, differentiation,
  heat flow, the dynamo seat.
- `surface.md` — the face: oceans, continents, ice, tectonics,
  day-night terminator, city lights.
- `atmosphere.md` — the blanket: layers, clouds, weather, climate,
  ozone, aurora layer.
- `moons.md` — the company: our Moon's shape, phases, and tides,
  small moons, rings, dust.
- `magnetosphere.md` — the shield: magnetic field, belts, day-night
  light, solar storms, auroras, habitability.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: solar-nebula condensation
  and accretion over millions of years to differentiation and the
  Moon-forming crash, ocean and air growth, oxidation and ice ages,
  life on land and the dinosaur-ending crash, humans and city lights,
  plus the far-future fate under a brightening Sun, with a reading
  guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a cross-check of
  aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`sun-fulldisk-sdo`,
`earth-fulldisk-nasa`), part close-ups (interior cut-away, Blue
Marble face, atmosphere rim, full Moon, aurora curtains), timeline
endpoint (Earthrise).
