# L08 — Planetary system

L08 = L8 (ladder row L8, Planetary system, 10^12–10^13 m). This
folder is the L8 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #225): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l7.md` — L7 behind us: what the Outer solar system
  looks like, what carries over into L8, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L7-to-L8 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `sun.md` — the Sun: disk and corona, activity and cycle, light
  and wind, habitable zone.
- `terrestrials.md` — inner rocky worlds: Mercury, Venus, Earth
  with its Moon, Mars.
- `giants.md` — outer giants as disks: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus,
  Neptune, with rings and large moons.
- `belts.md` — small-body populations: asteroid belt with Ceres,
  dwarf planets, Kuiper Belt at this zoom with Pluto and Arrokoth.
- `heliosphere.md` — the wind bubble: termination shock,
  heliosheath, heliopause near 120 AU with both Voyager crossings,
  preview toward L9.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: solar-nebula collapse
  over millions of years to planet formation and migration, belt
  sculpting and bombardment, the stable era with life, the Voyager
  crossing era, plus the red-giant and far-future fate, with a
  reading guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a cross-check
  of aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`oort-scale-infographic-nasa`,
`sun-full-disk-sdo`), part close-ups (Sun features, system
illustration, Saturn farewell, Arrokoth snowman, heliosphere
diagram), timeline endpoint (Pale Blue Dot).
