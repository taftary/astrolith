# L04 — Galaxies

L04 = L4 (ladder row L4, Galaxies, 10^20–10^21 m). This folder is the L4
visual and lifetime reference (Issue #221): what its parts look like,
how they were created, how they changed through time, and how they
interact — each page with target-visual images and named sources. The
bridge from the previous level and the entry transition come first; the
dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l3.md` — L3 behind us: what Galaxy clusters and groups look
  like, what carries over into L4, what changes at this zoom, key
  numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L3-to-L4 dive in plain steps: approach, effects and
  visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `spirals.md` — spiral galaxies: disks and arms, inside-out growth,
  quenching, Milky Way home with rotation-curve weighing.
- `ellipticals.md` — elliptical giants: smooth old glow, merger
  building, light profiles, M–sigma weighing, M87 home bridge.
- `dwarfs.md` — irregular and dwarf galaxies: LMC/SMC companions,
  bursts and tides, missing-satellites count, fossil ultra-faints.
- `nuclei.md` — galactic nuclei: central black holes, quasar era, duty
  cycle, Sgr A* home shadow and M87* portrait.
- `halos.md` — halos, gas and satellites: Enceladus building, the
  Splash, streams as fossils, globular tracers.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: cosmic dawn to cosmic noon
  to the Milky Way's building to today (13.787 Gyr) plus the merger and
  the far-future fate, with the HUDF reading guide and part
  cross-checks.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and license in
its doc. Bridge views (`andromeda-wide-hst`, `milkyway-panorama-eso0932a`),
part close-ups (Whirlpool spiral, full-galaxy M87, VISTA Large Cloud, Sgr A*
shadow, Omega Centauri, Hubble Ultra Deep Field).
