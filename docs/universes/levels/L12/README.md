# L12 — Cities and landscapes

L12 = L12 (ladder row L12, Cities and landscapes, 10^3–10^5 m). This
folder is the L12 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #229): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L12 continues the beyond-MVP tail: many cities per region, then buildings below.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l11.md` — L11 behind us: what the regions with continents and ranges
  look like, what carries over into L12, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L11-to-L12 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `cities.md` — the built mass: cities, downtown cores, districts,
  urban form and growth.
- `towns.md` — the spread: towns, suburbs, villages, sprawl,
  tracts and annexation.
- `networks.md` — the links: roads, rails, infrastructure corridors,
  blocks and interchanges.
- `farmland.md` — the patchwork: farmland, fields, countryside,
  sections and pivots.
- `landscapes.md` — the anchors: lakes, rivers at city scale, valleys,
  hills, forests and parks.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: first farms and ancient cities
  over thousands of years through medieval towns and industrial growth,
  sprawl and farmland change to today's cities, plus the far-future
  fate under continued growth, climate change and abandonment, with a reading
  guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a cross-check of
  aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`city-lights-nasa`,
`mexicali-city-farmland-nasa`), part close-ups (NYC night lights,
Monterrey city-mountain, Mexicali grids, farmland patchwork), timeline
endpoint (city lights global).
