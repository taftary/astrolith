- **URL:** [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundial)
- **Supports:**
  - A sundial consists of a flat plate (dial) and a gnomon that casts a shadow onto the dial.
  - The style is the time-telling edge of the gnomon; the shadow aligns with hour lines marked on the dial.
  - The style must be parallel to the Earth's rotation axis for year-round accuracy.
  - The style's angle from horizontal equals the sundial's geographic latitude.
