# References: research

Facts with takes. Interpretation in [analysis](./analysis.md); borrowing

## 1. Powers of Ten (Eames, 1977)

Nine-minute film from a Chicago lakeside picnic out to 10^24 m (100
million light-years) and back into the hand to 10^-16 m, zooming out at
one power of ten per 10 seconds and returning faster at about one per
2 seconds before slowing again [REF-S001]. Held by the Library of
Congress National Film Registry as culturally, historically, or
aesthetically significant [REF-S001].

- **Take:** continuous decade-step zoom with persistent scale narration
  (level plus distance always visible); fixed 10x steps make a 10^26
  range graspable, the direct model for the ladder plus scale bar.
- **Do not take:** the fixed linear 9-minute path with narration; the
  game needs player-controlled, distance-scaled, interruptible flights.

## 2. Out There (2014)

Roguelike resource survival on phones: explore, manage fuel, oxygen, and
hull (later morale), mine planets, jump system to system, permadeath at
zero [REF-S002]. Comic and graphic-novel art in a limited palette with a
lonely tone; TouchArcade 5 of 5, Metacritic iOS 85 [REF-S002].
Procedural galaxy with gamebook text choices [REF-S002].

- **Take:** three-meter survival tension with tap-to-jump plus event
  text is the closest HUD ancestor to fuel, oxygen, hull, plus heat;
  limited palette proves minimal art carries dread.
- **Do not take:** heavy randomness with brutal unfair deaths tied to
  lucky finds, text editing issues, and identical-planet repetition
  criticised in reviews [REF-S002]; Astrolith keeps deterministic
  readable meters with numbers and icons.

## 3. Kingdom Two Crowns (2018)

Side-scrolling micro-strategy: a mounted monarch spends coins to recruit,
build, and defend against nightly waves on a day-night cycle; crown loss
ends the run with an heir inheriting a damaged realm [REF-S003].
Two-tone silhouette modern pixel art in side view with minimalist feel;
praised strategy, art, and co-op, criticised slow pace; over 300,000
players in six months; phones from 2020 [REF-S003]. The creator chose the
style as the economical sketch-to-engine path where the viewer fills
detail [REF-S004].

- **Take:** one-input plus coin and meter minimalism plus silhouette
  readability works on phones; day-night plus single currency keeps
  chrome near zero, the model for four meters only.
- **Do not take:** the side-view builder loop, indirect villager control,
  and sedate rebuild pacing; Astrolith is scale and survival, not base
  defence.

## 4. FTL: Faster Than Light (Subset Games, 2012)

Roguelike ship simulation across eight procedural sectors with pausable
real-time combat, power routed to systems (piloting, engines, weapons,
oxygen, and more), hull damage, fire, breaches, and permadeath; the
top-down ship doubles as quasi-diegetic UI with status on top, left, and
bottom bars [REF-S005]. IGF Excellence in Design plus Audience Award,
Metacritic PC 84 and iPad 88 with praised touch controls [REF-S005]. The
postmortem describes iterating from one feeling ("captain a starship")
and cutting systems that did not serve it [REF-S006].

- **Take:** ship systems as UI plus pausable real-time plus power routing
  is the model for meters as systems with numbers; persistent ship state
  across jumps mirrors run structure; follow-the-feeling iteration is the
  method.
- **Do not take:** dense multi-panel desktop HUD with crew
  micromanagement, unreadable on phones; simplify to four meters plus
  scale plus distance; do not require pause to play.

## 5. Mobile game UI patterns

Frequent actions belong in the bottom-third thumb arc; top corners suit
read-only status; portrait and landscape need separate layouts; targets
stay 44 to 48 px minimum with status outside the input arc [REF-S007].
Safe areas govern placement on both platforms: lay out inside system
insets, never on physical edges [REF-S008]. Type scales stay systematic
with tabular numerals for changing numbers and minima at or above 11 pt
[REF-S009].

- **Take:** portrait-first Astrolith with status top-safe-area, meters
  compact, primary input bottom-thumb, separate landscape spec.
- **Do not take:** one stretched layout for both orientations; primary
  input in top corners; caption-size live numerals.
