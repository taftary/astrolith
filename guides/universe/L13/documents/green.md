# Green — parks, trees, gardens

This page is the L13 part doc for green spaces at 10–100 m:
parks, plazas, gardens, street trees, and champion trees —
the scale where one park with its paths, or one great tree
with its crown, fills the view. Forests at landscape scale
live in L12 (`forests-deserts.md`); the Mall's trees in
`civic.md:24-28`; street trees on the verges in
`entry.md:66`; rooms with plants in L14 (`layout.md` via
L14, and the planned occupants page).

From 100 m up, green reads as designed nature: paths
curving against the grid, lawns mown to the path edge,
canopy dotting plazas, one enormous crown where a champion
stands. Parks are the city's lungs drawn in plan — and its
thermometer: cool islands in the heat maps of L12
(`sky.md`).

## Parks and plazas

The 19th-century answer to the industrial city: large
landscapes breaking the grid — lawns, waters, rambles —
with perimeter apartments facing inward (`blocks.md:16`).
Civic greens differ: malls and memorial grounds with 2,000
elms and 3,000 cherry trees (`civic.md:27`), avenues as
procession routes, monuments as focal points. Plazas pave
the downtown version: fountains, vendors, lunch crowds,
parklets reclaiming parking lanes (`commercial.md:66`).
Rooftop gardens crown stadiums and garages
(`stadiums.md:76`); pocket parks fill single lots; greenways
thread railbeds and rivers into corridors. Maintenance is
the design: mowing lines, irrigation rings, leaf seasons,
replanting cycles — a park is gardening at city scale.

## Street trees and groves

Trees on the verges (`entry.md:66`, `previous-l12.md:83`)
turn streets into rooms: trunks as columns, crowns as
ceilings, shade dropping summer temperatures degrees.
Species by climate and salt tolerance, pits by sidewalk
width, roots versus pipes the eternal negotiation — the
urban forest managed tree by tree in municipal inventories.
Groves gather where campuses and cemeteries keep old
plantings; orchards and vineyards grid the rural edge
(L12 `farmland.md`).

## Champion trees

Individual giants: sequoias tens of meters tall and
millennia old, oaks spreading wider than houses, street
champions measured and fenced. A single crown fills an L13
frame entirely — trunk a tower, roots a foundation wider
than the canopy, leaf litter its own soil. Protection
follows fame: fences, lightning rods, irrigation in
drought, clones in arboreta. The step from champion tree
to forest stand looks up to L12 (`forests-deserts.md`).

## Gardens

Botanical collections labeled bed by bed; community plots
gridded with sheds and compost; courtyard gardens inside
blocks; healing gardens at hospitals (`civic.md`); kitchen
gardens behind houses (`houses.md`). Water decides:
irrigation rings, rain gardens catching roof runoff,
xeriscapes where supply fails. Gardens are the smallest
parks and the largest rooms — the scale where L13 meets
L14 planting.

## How it interacts with the others

Houses face (`houses.md`): park-facing premiums, garden
suburbs, backyard plots. Blocks vent (`blocks.md`):
courtyards, setbacks, roof gardens. Commerce spills
(`commercial.md`): cafe terraces, market squares, parklet
seating. Civic processes (`civic.md`): malls, memorials,
arboreta. Industry buffers (`industrial.md`): shelter
belts, remediation plantings. Stadiums ring (`stadiums.md`):
approach lawns, tailgate groves. Sacred plants
(`religious.md`): temple groves, churchyards, cemetery
trees. Spans cross (`structures.md`): parks under bridges,
trails on towpaths. Stations arrive (`transport.md`):
plazas, lawns, planted garage roofs. Towns keep their
greens (L12 `towns.md`); forests stand beyond (L12
`forests-deserts.md`).

## Key numbers

- Parks: grid-breaking lawns; malls with thousands of
named trees; plazas paved for crowds.
- Street trees: trunks as columns; species by salt;
roots versus pipes.
- Champions: tens of meters, millennia old; crowns
filling a frame.
- Gardens: beds, plots, courts — L13 meeting L14.
- Maintenance is design: mowing, rings, seasons, cycles.
- L13 frame: 10–100 m — one park or one crown per view.
