# L13 — Buildings

L13 = L13 (ladder row L13, Buildings, 10^1–10^2 m). This
folder is the L13 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #230): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L13 continues the beyond-MVP tail: many buildings per city, then rooms below.
Start at the bridge, dive through entry, pick any part, then confirm
dates in the lifetime — and continue down to
[L14](../../L14/documents/README.md) for rooms per building.

## Scope and scale

L13 covers buildings of one city: 10–100 m patches in the
10^1–10^2 m range around houses, blocks, shops, civic halls, works,
and stadium bowls — about 0.000008 of the 12,756 km planet
width at 100 m, about 0.0000008 at 10 m. It sits between the
[L12 — Cities and landscapes](../../L12/documents/README.md) above and the
[L14 — Room](../../L14/documents/README.md) below,
under the [Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder.
Frozen scale rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder
holds the descriptive reference only. The worked example is Earth
buildings seen by astronauts and survey imagers; other worlds appear
only as comparative notes in part docs.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l12.md](./previous-l12.md)) first, then
the entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below,
then the dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind
each claim.

## Parts

- [houses.md](./houses.md) — the rows: houses, residential buildings, yards,
  lots and subdivisions.
- [blocks.md](./blocks.md) — the walls: apartment and office blocks, mid- and high-rise,
  curtain walls and conversions.
- [commercial.md](./commercial.md) — the glass: shops, offices, commercial cores,
  malls and big boxes.
- [civic.md](./civic.md) — the shared: schools, hospitals, civic buildings,
  campuses and precincts.
- [industrial.md](./industrial.md) — the made: warehouses, factories, sheds,
  docks and chimneys.
- [stadiums.md](./stadiums.md) — the crowds: stadiums, arenas, large-span structures,
  bowls and roofs.

One block runs through all six: rows set the homes, walls set the
mass, glass sets the trade, halls set the shared and made, bowls set
the crowds with the street-block fabric — the dated version of this
story runs through [lifetime.md](./lifetime.md).

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only
the navigation-level numbers: L13 span 10^1–10^2 m (buildings 10–100 m
across); houses about 15 m with a 2,142 sq ft median floor area (2025)
on lots near 8,543 sq ft; street lanes about 10 ft with residential
streets 32–40 ft paved in a 50 ft right-of-way; tall from 14 stories
or 50 m with 173 supertalls worldwide; stadium bowls hundreds of m
across (Astrodome 642 ft clear span, Wembley 315 m arch, Brasilia
55,155 m2 membrane); L12-to-L13 leg is short (one to two decades,
quiet magnification; open 0.14 rad, close 0.10 rad, preview from
0.02 rad, at most 6 markers) —
see the [L12 bridge](./previous-l12.md#key-numbers), the [entry
numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the dated
[timeline](./lifetime.md#cross-check-of-aligned-timescales).

## How this level differs from L12

[L12](../../L12/documents/README.md) frames one city — a 1 to
100 km patch with its mass, spread, lines, patchwork, and anchors,
where a single house is far below the zoom. Here one patch of that
city fills the view: the counted urban order falls back into
surroundings (downtown axis as street lines, highway path as lanes,
park and water tint as yards and verges) while houses, blocks,
commercial cores, civic campuses, works, and stadium bowls take center
as the rows, the walls, the glass, the shared, the made, and the
crowds — so new house, tower, shop, school, shed, and bowl results
enter through the part docs, not this index.
