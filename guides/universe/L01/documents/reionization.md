# Reionization — the universe lights up

This page is the L01 part doc for the Dark Ages to cosmic
dawn to ionized universe: the visual epoch other pages
only table. Rows live in `cosmic-web.md:84-93` and
`lifetime.md:25,62-64`; 21-cm "in principle" notes in
`lifetime.md`; IGM opacity in L02 (future `igm.md`); dwarf
galaxies in L04 (`dwarfs.md`, `lifetime.md`); the timeline
spine in `lifetime.md`.

For a hundred million years after the Big Bang there was
nothing to see: hydrogen fog, no stars, the CMB cooling
through infrared. Then the first stars ignited, their
ultraviolet chewing bubbles of ionized gas that grew,
merged, and cleared the fog by redshift ~6 — the universe
becoming transparent. Every deep field since looks through
the cleared air; reionization is the story of the clearing.

## Dark Ages — the fog

Recombination (see `background.md`) freed the CMB and
locked hydrogen neutral; gravity gathered the dark-matter
scaffolding unseen. No light but the cooling afterglow —
21-cm hydrogen line glowing faintly against it, the signal
radio arrays now hunt. Hundred-million-year night, ending
one star at a time.

## Cosmic dawn — first light

First stars (Pop III in `lifetime.md:63`): massive,
metal-free, brief — living millions of years and dying as
supernovae and black holes, seeding metals and holes.
First galaxies: dwarfs assembling, JWST now finding them
bright and early — reionizing photons leaking from
thousands of dwarfs, not a few giants. Black-hole seeds
growing toward quasars (see L04 `nuclei.md` quasar era).

## Bubbles to transparency — the clearing

Ionized bubbles grew around sources, Gunn-Peterson troughs
marking where fog remained: complete troughs in z>6
quasars (2001 SDSS z=6.28: flux down 150x — yet 10^-4
neutral suffices for black, so troughs date the end, not
the middle). Patchy end ~6–7 (JWST galaxy troughs now map
it source by source, 2025); helium's second reionization
by quasars finishing ~3. Timeline table in
`lifetime.md:62-64`; IGM physics in L02 `igm.md`.

## 21-cm cosmology — hearing the fog

Hydrogen's 21-cm line redshifts to meters: mapping the
neutral gas directly through the Dark Ages and dawn —
tomography of the invisible era. Sought by radio arrays
(EDGES claimed a dawn dip, debated; HERA and SKA mapping
onward); foregrounds a thousand times brighter are the
fight. Detection would date first stars to millions of
years and test the standard story end to end.

## Key numbers

- Dark Ages ~100 Myr; first stars massive, brief, metal-free.
- Bubbles merging; complete by z~6–7; 2001 trough at 6.28.
- 10^-4 neutral blacks a trough; He II done ~3.
- 21-cm: meters wavelength, foregrounds 1000x brighter.
- L01 frame: ~10^27 m — the whole clearing per view.

Sources: in-repo pages; JHU trough detection summary.

## Example objects

The z=6.28 quasar is the trough standard: flux down 150x —
fog's last wall.

JWST dwarfs are the clearer standard: bright and early —
thousands leaking light.

The 21-cm line is the hearing standard: meters long —
tomography of night.

Helium at z~3 is the finish standard: quasars completing —
second clearing done.
