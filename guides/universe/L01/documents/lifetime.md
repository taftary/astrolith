# Lifetime of the universe

Timeline doc for Issue #211 (loop iteration 7). The full dated story the
whole-view and part docs hang on: Big Bang to present plus far-future fate,
Planck 2018 baseline (13.787 Gyr). Alternates flagged inline. Sources per
section; full list at the bottom.

## Key anchors

- Inflation: 10^-36-10^-32 s, expansion by ~10^26-10^30 (e-folds
  convention), quantum seeds stretched to cosmic scales.
- Matter-radiation equality: ~47-50 kyr (z~3400-3600) — perturbations can
  grow.
- Recombination / CMB release: 290-370 kyr (peak ~372-380 kyr by definition),
  z~1090-1100, ~3000-4000 K — photons free-stream, baryons ~5 x 10^8
  atoms/m^3.
- Dark Ages: 0.37 Myr-150 Myr (fully ends ~1 Gyr), no stars, 21-cm only.
- First stars: 100-400 Myr (z~30-12), ~100 solar-mass metal-free; JWST
  MoM-z14 at z=14.44.
- Reionization: 200 Myr-1 Gyr (z 20-6); dwarf galaxies likely main drivers.
- Cosmic noon: 2-6 Gyr (peak z~2), star formation ~10x present;
  protoclusters (z=6) to clusters (z=2.1) to superclusters (z=1.2).
- Lambda domination: ~9.8 Gyr (z~0.4-0.66 by convention) — acceleration
  starts; Solar System forms alongside.
- Present: 13.787 Gyr, 2.725 K, flat, ~68% dark energy — Stelliferous Era.

Sources: chronology of the universe; ESA cosmic eras; Planck 2018 (arXiv
1807.06209); NASA dark energy; NASA Webb early universe.

## Caveats (alternates on the record)

- Age: 13.787 (Planck 2018) vs 13.772 (WMAP9) vs 13.798-13.813 (Planck
  2013/2015) — dataset combos; H0 67.4 vs ~73 shifts the prior.
- Recombination: 370 kyr tabular vs 375 WMAP vs 380 ESA vs 372 Planck peak —
  start vs peak vs complete definitions; z 1089-1100 standard, 1270 helium
  tail.
- Inflation factor 10^26 vs 10^30 (even 10^78 volume forms) — e-folds
  convention, order-of-magnitude physics.
- Equality 47 vs 60 kyr — matter-density assumption.
- Observable size 93 vs 94 Gly — rounding.
- BAO ruler 147-150 Mpc — Planck vs SDSS calibration.
- Web fractions vary factors of 2-5 by finder — always cite method.
- Largest structure: Quipu 400 Mpc confirmed vs Hercules-Corona Borealis
  3 Gpc disputed — incomparable tracers.
- Far-future dates are order-of-magnitude (Adams-Laughlin), assume constant
  Lambda and often proton decay.
