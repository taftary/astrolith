# Lifetime of the universe

Timeline doc for Issue #211 (loop iteration 10). The full dated story the
whole-view and part docs hang on: Big Bang to present plus far-future fate.
Baseline is Planck 2018 flat Lambda-CDM: age 13.787 +/- 0.020 Gyr,
H0 = 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc, budget ~4.9% ordinary / ~26.5% cold dark
matter / ~68.5% dark energy. Alternates flagged inline, including the
local H0 ~70-73 tension that shifts age priors by ~1%. Sources per
section; full list at the bottom.

This document extends the [cosmic-web overview](./cosmic-web.md) with dates
and the [background](./background.md) with the expansion history; sibling
part docs (`voids.md`, `filaments.md`, `walls.md`, `nodes.md`,
`superclusters.md`) hang dated events off this spine.

## Key anchors

- Inflation: 10^-36-10^-32 s, size grows by ~10^30 (at least e^60);
  minute quantum ripples amplified to cosmological density seeds.
- Matter-radiation equality: ~47-50 kyr (z~3400-3600) — dark matter
  gathers first, perturbations can grow.
- Recombination / CMB release: 290-370 kyr (peak ~372-380 kyr by
  definition), z~1090-1100, ~3000 K — electrons and protons bind, photons
  free-stream to today's 2.725 K glow (~411 per cm^3).
- Dark Ages: 0.37 Myr-150 Myr (fully ends ~1 Gyr), no stars, 21-cm only.
- First stars: 100-400 Myr (z~30-12), 30-300 solar masses per theory;
  JWST MoM-z14 at z=14.44 (~280 Myr).
- Reionization: 200 Myr-1 Gyr (z 20-6); ultra-faint dwarfs (4x assumed UV)
  likely main drivers; midpoint z_re = 7.68.
- Cosmic noon: 2-6 Gyr (peak z~2), star formation ~10x present; half of
  all stellar mass forms in ~2-3 Gyr; protoclusters (z=6) to clusters
  (z=2.1) to superclusters (z=1.2).
- Lambda domination: ~9.8 Gyr (z~0.4-0.66 by convention, a ~ 0.6) —
  acceleration starts; Solar System forms alongside.
- Present: 13.787 Gyr, 2.725 K, flat to <1%, ~68% dark energy —
  Stelliferous Era; DESI DR1-DR2 plus Euclid map it.

Sources: chronology of the universe; ESA cosmic eras; ESA Planck CMB
pages; Planck 2018 (arXiv 1807.06209); NASA dark energy; NASA building
blocks; NASA Webb early universe; Webb dwarf-galaxy reionization;
DESI DR2 guide.

All times are Lambda-CDM lookback conventions; temperatures and redshifts
are approximate. Radiation era runs inflation to ~47 kyr; matter era
~47 kyr to ~9.8 Gyr; Lambda era after ~9.8 Gyr.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Reheating / Hot Big Bang | ~10^-32 s | — | to ~10^23-10^27 K | Inflaton energy transfers to matter and light; hot plasma era begins (NASA Webb early-universe: energy driving inflation becomes matter and light) |

## Branch C: alternative fates

Baseline above is Big Freeze / Heat Death (constant Lambda, NASA
building-blocks budget 5/27/68). Each branch below names its trigger and
what would replace the baseline; none is confirmed.

| Scenario | When if triggered | Trigger | End state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big Freeze (baseline) | >10^100 yr | Constant Lambda (w = -1); NASA 68% dark energy holds | Heat Death / Dark Era: maximum entropy, uniform T, no work possible |
| Big Rip | 22 Gyr hence (w=-1.5) to never (w=-1) | Phantom dark energy (w<-1) growing with expansion | Horizon shrinks past clusters, galaxies, atoms in sequence to infinity |
| Big Crunch | no date | Closed universe or dark energy turning attractive (quintessence decay) | Halt, reverse, hot dense singularity; possibly cyclic bounce |
| Big Bounce (cyclic) | no date | Crunch followed by new expansion (loop/ekpyrotic variants) | New Hot Big Bang; debated, no observable support |
| Big Slurp (vacuum decay) | 20-30 Gyr estimate, or anytime | Metastable Higgs vacuum tunneling | Light-speed bubble rewrites physics everywhere |
| No-proton-decay | iron stars 10^1500 yr | Stable protons | Everything tunnels to iron, then holes, then dark era — longer |

DESI DR1-DR2 (2024-2025) evolving-dark-energy hints (up to ~4.2 sigma
with supernova combos, below the 5-sigma discovery threshold) keep the
branches open. Sources:
Big Rip; ultimate fate; future of an expanding universe.

## Caveats (alternates on the record)

- Age: 13.787 (Planck 2018) vs 13.772 (WMAP9) vs 13.798-13.813 (Planck
  2013/2015) — dataset combos; H0 67.4 vs ~73 shifts the prior.
  Full Planck 2018: Omega_c h^2 = 0.120, Omega_b h^2 = 0.0224,
  n_s = 0.965, tau = 0.054, N_eff = 2.99, sum m_nu < 0.12 eV.
- Recombination: 370 kyr tabular vs 375 WMAP vs 380 ESA vs 372 Planck peak —
  start vs peak vs complete definitions; z 1089-1100 standard, 1270 helium
  tail.
- Inflation factor 10^26 vs 10^30 (even 10^78 volume forms) — e-folds
  convention, order-of-magnitude physics; ESA Planck quotes ~10^30.
- Equality 47 vs 60 kyr — matter-density assumption.
- Observable size 93 vs 94 Gly — rounding.
- BAO ruler 147-150 Mpc — Planck vs SDSS calibration; DESI DR2 reaches
  0.24% overall.
- Web fractions vary factors of 2-5 by finder — always cite method.
- Largest structure: Quipu 400 Mpc confirmed vs Hercules-Corona Borealis
  3 Gpc disputed — incomparable tracers.
- Far-future dates are order-of-magnitude (Adams-Laughlin), assume constant
  Lambda and often proton decay; DESI evolving-dark-energy hints
  (robust at z<0.3 per extended analysis, still <5 sigma) could revise them.
- H0 tension exceeds 4-5 sigma between Planck 67.4 and local ~73 ladders;
  Webb lensed-supernova SN H0pe confirms the tension rather than
  resolving it.
