# L05 — Galactic structures

L05 = L5 (ladder row L5, Galactic structures, 10^17–10^19 m). This folder
is the L5 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #222): what its parts look
like, how they were created, how they changed through time, and how they
interact — each page with target-visual images and named sources. The
bridge from the previous level and the entry transition come first; the
dated timeline runs through everything.

## Scope and scale

L05 covers one cell of galactic geography: spiral arms, giant molecular
clouds, open and globular clusters, and the disk, bulge, bar, and diffuse
gas that holds them, in the ~3–300-parsec range, anchored on the
molecular-cloud complex at 100 parsecs (3.09 x 10^18 m). It sits between
the [L04 — Galaxies](../../L04/documents/README.md) above and the
[L06 — Stellar neighborhood](../../L06/documents/README.md) below, under
the [Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder. Frozen scale
rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder holds the
descriptive reference only.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l4.md](./previous-l4.md)) first, then the
entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below, then the
dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind each claim.

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only the
navigation-level numbers: L5 span 10^17–10^19 m; anchor molecular-cloud
complex 100 parsecs (3.09 x 10^18 m); Sun ~26,000 light-years (~8 kpc)
from the center on the inner edge of the Orion Spur (~20,000 light-years
long, ~3,000 light-years wide); Eagle Nebula ~7,000 light-years out (SIMBAD-based
estimates run ~5,700), Pleiades ~444 light-years, Orion complex
1,000–1,400 light-years, Carina ~8,500 light-years, 47 Tuc ~14,500
light-years, Crab remnant 6,500 light-years away and 6 light-years wide;
cloud lives ~10–30 million years at ~1–2 percent efficiency, open-cluster
half-lives 150–800 million years, globular survivors 11–13 billion years;
L4-to-L5 ratio 3.24e-3 with 32 markers per L4 cell (2.49 decades,
e 20.98 to e 18.49) — see [key numbers](./previous-l4.md#key-numbers),
[entry numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the
[dated timeline](./lifetime.md).

## How this level differs from L04

[L04](../../L04/documents/README.md) shows the island — one whole galaxy
with a type label, a disk, a bulge, a nucleus, and a halo, swimming among
companions. Here one member is the terrain: the disk that looked smooth
from outside opens into winding arms edged with dust, clouds glowing
inside them, young clusters hatching and dispersing over hundreds of
millions of years while ancient globulars keep their 12-billion-year
record overhead. The galaxy becomes surroundings — the coin you came down
stops being an object and starts being everywhere, because you are inside
it. The sorting rule arrives with the weather: arms crowd gas into clouds,
clouds hatch clusters, clusters feed the field, the disk frame holds the
line — so new Gaia, PHANGS, and cluster-census results enter through the
part docs, not this index.
