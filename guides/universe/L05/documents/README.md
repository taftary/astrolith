# L05 — Galactic structures

L05 = L5 (ladder row L5, Galactic structures, 10^17–10^19 m). This folder
is the L5 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #222): what its parts look
like, how they were created, how they changed through time, and how they
interact — each page with target-visual images and named sources. The
bridge from the previous level and the entry transition come first; the
dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l4.md` — L4 behind us: what Galaxies look like, what carries
  over into L5, what changes at this zoom, key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L4-to-L5 dive in plain steps: approach, effects and
  visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `arms.md` — spiral arms: density ridges, dust lanes, dynamic vs steady
  picture, Orion Spur home with NGC 1232 face-on reference.
- `clouds.md` — giant molecular clouds and star-forming regions: Orion and
  Carina showcase, collapse and feedback, Larson rules and PHANGS census.
- `open.md` — open clusters: Pleiades youth, Hyades middle age, mass
  segregation and tidal dispersal into the field.
- `globulars.md` — globular clusters: 47 Tuc home example, multiple
  populations, halo orbits as assembly fossils.
- `disk.md` — disk, bulge, bar plus diffuse gas and halo: thin/thick disk,
  peanut bulge, superbubbles and fountains, the Gaia Snail.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: cloud assembly over tens of
  millions of years to protostar collapse, feedback and emergence,
  cluster dispersal over hundreds of millions of years, recycling, the
  11–13-billion-year globular survivors, and the far-future fate with a
  reading guide mapping each stage to its part doc.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and license
in its doc. Bridge views (`eagle-nebula-eso0926a`, `pleiades-eso-b11`),
part close-ups (NGC 1232 arms, Carina Nebula, Wild Duck Cluster, 47 Tuc,
edge-on NGC 891), timeline endpoint (Crab Nebula remnant).
