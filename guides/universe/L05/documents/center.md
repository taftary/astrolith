# Center — the downtown 500 parsecs

This page is the L05 part doc for the Galactic Center at
L05 scale: the Central Molecular Zone, nuclear star
cluster, Arches and Quintuplet clusters, Sgr B2, and the
Fermi-bubble bases — everything around the black hole
except the hole itself. Sgr A* stays canonical in L04
(`nuclei.md`); bubbles in `disk.md:144-147`; arm context
in `arms.md:188-189`; the bridge nod in
`previous-l4.md:117`.

Every galaxy has a downtown where densities run a million
times the suburbs: gas piled deep, stars packed tight,
clusters massive and young, a four-million-sun hole
holding court. Ours is 26,000 light-years away behind dust
— invisible optically, blazing in infrared, radio, X-ray,
and gamma. One L05 frame holds the whole inner 500
parsecs: the most extreme star formation in the galaxy,
arguing distance and all.

## The Central Molecular Zone

A lopsided ring of dense gas ~500 parsecs across holding
tens of millions of solar masses — a few percent of the
galaxy's gas in a hundredth of its volume. Sgr B2, the
largest molecular cloud complex in the galaxy, brews
complex organics; the Brick (G0.253) refuses to form
stars despite its mass — turbulence holding collapse at
bay, the exception testing every star-formation rule in
`clouds.md`. Inflow runs along the bar (`disk.md:40-58`);
starbursts flicker on million-year timescales.

## The nuclear star cluster and disk

Four million suns of hole wrapped in tens of millions of
suns of stars: the nuclear cluster — densest in the galaxy,
old and young mixed, S-stars whipping around Sgr A* on
decade orbits — inside a flattened nuclear disk of ongoing
formation. Tides shred infalling clouds into streamers;
the circumnuclear ring feeds the hole at a trickle today,
feast in the past.

## Arches and Quintuplet — the massive young

Two clusters, millions of years old, hundreds of the most
massive stars known: Arches (densest young cluster in the
galaxy) and Quintuplet (with the Pistol Star shedding
nebulae). Prototypes of starburst-cluster birth —
Westerlund 1 and R136 in `open.md:174` are their suburban
cousins. Winds and coming supernovae will unbind both;
their mass function tests whether downtown makes stars
differently (top-heavy hints persist).

## Sgr A East and the bubble bases

Sgr A East: the downtown supernova remnant wrapping the
center (see `supernovae.md` for the class). Above and
below, the Fermi/eROSITA bubbles rise 25,000 light-years
(see `disk.md:144-147`) — past black-hole feasts or
starburst winds, still debated; their bases glow where
they meet the CMZ. The center connects to the whole
galaxy through these chimneys.

## How it interacts with the others

Arms feed (`arms.md`): bar-driven inflow supplying the
ring. Clouds compare (`clouds.md`, `nebulae.md`): Brick
versus Orion — turbulence versus collapse. Clusters echo
(`open.md`, `globulars.md`): Arches/Quintuplet as massive
youth, nuclear cluster as downtown globular. Disk vents
(`disk.md`): bubbles, fountains, corona from downtown
outbursts. Remnants mark (`supernovae.md`): Sgr A East and
coming cluster supernovae. The hole reigns (L04
`nuclei.md`): S-stars, flares, EHT shadow. Lifetimes run
in `lifetime.md`; neighborhoods receive ejecta in L06.

Target visual: a dedicated downtown frame (Sgr A region
multi-wavelength, NASA — link, not copy) is proposed; no
new raster file is added here.

## Key numbers

- CMZ ~500 pc, tens of millions of solar masses; Sgr B2
largest cloud complex; Brick starless despite mass.
- Nuclear cluster tens of millions of Suns around 4M-Sun
Sgr A*; S-stars on decade orbits.
- Arches/Quintuplet: Myr-old, 100s of massive stars.
- Bubbles 25,000 ly up; bases at the CMZ.
- L05 frame: 10^17–10^19 m — downtown whole per view.

and Fermi-bubble pages.

## Example objects

Sgr B2 is the cauldron standard: largest complex —
organics brewing.

The Brick is the stubborn standard: massive, starless —
turbulence winning.

Arches is the youth standard: densest young cluster —
downtown birth.

Sgr A East is the remnant standard: blast wrapping the
hole — downtown death.
