# Stairs and thresholds — the vertical inches

This page is the L14 part doc for stairs, landings,
railings, and thresholds: rise, run, rails, and the strips
between inside and out. Shell edges live in `shell.md`;
circulation in `layout.md`; clearances in `furniture.md`;
the dated stair (Willey quote, Biltmore count) in
`lifetime.md`.

Stairs are the room folded vertical: each step a small
floor, each flight a corridor standing up. Get the inches
wrong and bodies fall — more injuries happen on stairs
than on any other building part — so the rules are written
in blood and building code: risers capped, treads
guaranteed, rails gripped, landings resting, nosings
visible.

## Rise and run

The code scrive: maximum 7¾-inch riser, minimum 10-inch
tread (IRC R311.7), steepest safe rhythm feet learned —
deviate and toes catch or heels slip. Winders taper and
trap; spiral kits save space and spill the unwary; alternating-
tread ships' ladders fit lofts where stairs cannot. Nosings
overhang an inch to catch the eye and the toe; contrast
strips mark edges for dim eyes. Outdoor steps widen and
roughen: freeze, leaf, and moss rewrite friction seasonally.

## Landings, rails, guards

Flights rest at landings every ~12 ft of vertical (and at
every door swing and turn): 36-inch minimum depth, the
stair's punctuation and the furniture's turning bay —
no sofa wider than its landing arrives. Handrails both
sides at 34–38 inches, grippable (not 2x4 flat), continuous
through winders; guards where drops exceed 30 inches, balusters
spaced so a 4-inch sphere cannot pass — the toddler test.
Lighting: switches top and bottom (three-way), steps lit,
never dark.

## Thresholds — the strips between

Door saddles, shower curbs, balcony tracks, garage aprons:
each a small dam against water, air, and dirt — and each a
trip or a barrier. Zero-threshold showers grade the floor
instead (`wet-rooms.md`); ramps at 1:12 replace steps where
wheels rule (`layout.md` ADA clearances); weatherstripped
saddles seal the envelope (`shell.md`). Every threshold
chooses: keep out, or let in.

## Key numbers

- Riser max 7¾ in, tread min 10 in (IRC R311.7); nosing
~1 in; rails 34–38 in grippable.
- Landings 36 in+ deep every ~12 ft vertical and at turns.
- Guards past 30-in drops; balusters pass no 4-in sphere.
- Switches top and bottom; steps lit, never dark.
- Ramps 1:12 where wheels rule; zero entries where water
grades.
- L14 frame: 1–10 m — one flight with landings per view.
