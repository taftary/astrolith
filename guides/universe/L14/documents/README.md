# L14 — Room

L14 = L14 (ladder row L14, Room, 10^0–10^1 m). This
folder is the L14 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #231): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L14 ends the beyond-MVP tail at the 1 m end point: many rooms per building, then nothing below.
Start at the bridge, dive through entry, pick any part, then confirm
dates in the lifetime.

## Scope and scale

L14 covers rooms of one building: 1–10 m patches in the
10^0–10^1 m range around bedrooms, parlors, kitchens, halls, and
laboratory modules — about 0.0000008 of the 12,756 km planet
width at 10 m, about 0.00000008 at 1 m. It sits between the
[L13 — Buildings](../../L13/documents/README.md) above and nothing
below — the last rung — under the [Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder.
Frozen scale rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder
holds the descriptive reference only. The worked example is Earth
rooms seen in place and survey photography; other worlds appear
only as comparative notes in part docs.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l13.md](./previous-l13.md)) first, then
the entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below,
then the dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind
each claim.

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only
the navigation-level numbers: L14 span 10^0–10^1 m (rooms 1–10 m
across, decades e 0–1); habitable rooms at least 70 sq ft with 7 ft
in every direction, ceilings conventionally 8 ft; bedrooms about
11 by 12 ft; interior doors 30 by 80 in stock, hallways 36 in
minimum; queen beds 60 by 80 in, dining tops at 28–30 in with
17–19 in seats, counters finished at 36 in with wall cabinets
12 in deep hung 18 in above; reach band 15–48 in, work-triangle
legs 4–9 ft totaling at most 26 ft; rugs 8-by-10 most-bought,
9-by-12 holding all four legs; L13-to-L14 leg is short (about one
decade, quiet magnification; open 0.14 rad, close 0.10 rad,
preview from 0.02 rad, at most 6 markers) —
see the [L13 bridge](./previous-l13.md#key-numbers), the [entry
numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the dated
[timeline](./lifetime.md#timescale-cross-check).

## How this level differs from L13

[L13](../../L13/documents/README.md) frames one city patch — a 10 to
100 m view with its rows, walls, glass, shared halls, works, and
bowls, where a single chair is far below the zoom. Here one chamber
of one building fills the view: the counted building order falls back
into surroundings (exterior wall as interior finish, roof as ceiling,
block as circulation) while floors, walls, ceilings, doors, beds,
chairs, shelves, lamps, rugs, baths, machines, stairs, bodies, and
racks take center as the enclosure, the
use, the keeping, the served, the soft, the plan, the plumbed, the
machines, the vertical inches, the living contents, and the weightless
rooms — so new shell,
furniture, cabinet, lamp, textile, layout, bath, appliance, stair,
occupant, and orbital results enter through
the part docs, not this index.
