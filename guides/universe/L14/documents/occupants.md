# Occupants — people, pets, plants

This page is the L14 part doc for the living contents at
1–2 m: human bodies behind every clearance, pets, and
houseplants — the scale where the room's users fill the
view. Clearances imply them in `furniture.md:93`,
`layout.md`, and `storage.md:105`; desks and electronics
in `furniture.md:20-23`; planting at building scale in L13
`green.md`.

Every inch in these guides is a body: seat heights 17–19
inches are thighs, counters are elbows, shelves are
reaches, doorways are shoulders with bags. Design for the
range — 5th-percentile reach to 95th-percentile clearance,
children to elders, canes to chairs — and the room fits
all; design for the average and it fits none.

Occupants off Earth: astronauts (see `orbital-rooms.md`) — bodies
negotiating weightlessness instead of stairs.

## People — the measurements

Standing reach, seated eye, elbow, knee, hip: counters at
elbow minus a hand, tables at 28–30 inches, seats 17–19,
mirrors at standing eye, controls where seated hands find
them (`appliances.md`). Turning needs a 60-inch circle
(ADA); corridors pass at 36, pass wheelchairs at 60;
doors clear 32 minimum. Children climb everything (guards
in `stairs-thresholds.md`); elders need light, contrast,
grab bars, and zero thresholds (`wet-rooms.md`); sleep
needs dark and quiet (`textiles-decor.md` blackout,
`shell.md` mass). Beds, baths, and desks are body
instruments first, furniture second.

## Pets — the low occupants

Dogs, cats, birds, tanks: doors with pet flaps, crates in
mudrooms, litter behind cabinet doors, tanks on
weight-bearing walls (water weighs — screws in hollow
drywall fail, see `shell.md`). Scratching posts save
sofas; washable throws save both (`textiles-decor.md`);
fenced yards extend the room (L13 `houses.md`). Allergies
and vacuums negotiate fur; aquariums humidify winter air.
Pets rewrite circulation: gates on stairs, ramps for old
hips, rooms arranged around the animal's sun spot.

## Plants — the green occupants

Light first: south windows for sun-lovers, north for
ferns, grow lights where neither suffices. Water second:
pots with drainage, saucers, moisture meters beating
schedules. Soil, repotting, and pests third. Big plants
are furniture — fiddle figs dividing rooms, trees in
atria; herbs are crops — kitchen sills feeding dinner.
Plants clean air slowly and moods quickly; they also drop
leaves into drains, harbor fungus gnats, and die
informatively — every brown tip a sensor reading of
light, water, or heat (`light-power.md` registers and
radiators).

## How it interacts with the others

Shell fits (`shell.md`): doors 32+ clear, glass at eye and
knee, anchors in studs for tanks and mounts. Furniture
serves (`furniture.md`): seats, desks, beds as body
instruments. Storage reaches (`storage.md`): 5th-percentile
shelves, 95th-percentile clearance. Light and power
follow (`light-power.md`): task light at benches, outlets
by beds, registers away from leaves. Textiles comfort
(`textiles-decor.md`): washable throws, blackout for
sleep, runners pinned. Layout circulates (`layout.md`):
60-inch turns, 36-inch passes, zones by noise. Wet rooms
adapt (`wet-rooms.md`): bars, benches, hand showers.
Appliances answer (`appliances.md`): controls at seated
and standing heights. Stairs test (`stairs-thresholds.md`):
knees, toddlers, canes on every flight.

- Turning 60-in circle; corridors 36 pass / 60 wheelchairs;
doors 32+ clear.
- Controls at seated and standing hands; mirrors at eyes.
- Pets: flaps, crates, tanks on studs; plants: light,

- Design 5th-percentile reach to 95th-percentile clearance.
- L14 frame: 1–2 m — the occupants themselves per view.
