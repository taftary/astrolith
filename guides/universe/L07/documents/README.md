# L07 — Outer solar system

L07 = L7 (ladder row L7, Outer solar system, 10^15–10^16 m). This
folder is the L7 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #224): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Scope and scale

L07 covers one outer solar system: the giant-planets zone, the
Kuiper Belt doughnut, the scattered disc and detached objects, the
spherical Oort reservoir, and the comet traffic with the heliosphere
edge, in the ~40–400-light-day range (10^15–10^16 m), anchored on
the Oort cloud outer edge at 100,000 AU (1.50 x 10^16 m). It sits
between the [L06 — Stellar
neighborhood](../../L06/documents/README.md) above and the [L08 —
Planetary system](../../L08/documents/README.md) below, under the
[Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder. Frozen scale
rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder holds the
descriptive reference only.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l6.md](./previous-l6.md)) first, then
the entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below,
then the dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind
each claim.

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only
the navigation-level numbers: L7 span 10^15–10^16 m; anchor Oort
cloud outer edge 100,000 AU (1.50 x 10^16 m); giants at Jupiter
5.2 AU, Saturn ~9.5 AU, Uranus ~19 AU, Neptune ~30 AU; Kuiper
Belt 30–50 AU with detached Sedna closest ~76 AU; termination shock
80–100 AU and heliopause near 120 AU (Voyager 1 at 94 AU in 2004
and 122 AU in 2012, Voyager 2 at 84 AU in 2007 and 119 AU in
2018); Hills cloud ~2,000–20,000 AU with the outer sphere to
~100,000 AU; L6-to-L7 ratio 3.55e-1 with at most 6 markers per L6
cell (usually one) and no zoom gap, L7-to-L8 ratio 1.20e-3 with 32
markers per L7 cell and two zoom gaps — see [key
numbers](./previous-l6.md#key-numbers), [entry
numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the [dated
timeline](./lifetime.md).

## How this level differs from L06

[L06](../../L06/documents/README.md) counts hearths — hundreds of
neighbor suns light-years apart, bound pairs and triples, cooling
embers, drifting through the thin Local Bubble cavity. Here one of
those hearths resolves into shells: the counted suns fall back into
background dots while a single yellow dwarf takes center with its
giant planets, its Kuiper doughnut, its scattered and detached
strays, its spherical Oort reservoir, and the comet traffic crossing
them — so new belt-census, visitor, and boundary-crossing results
enter through the part docs, not this index.
