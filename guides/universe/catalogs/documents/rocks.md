# Rock kinds — every stone detailed

This catalog details rock, meteorite, ice, and mineral
kinds: look, makeup, where each forms, ladder location,
examples. All **observed** (classes from samples and
spectra) unless tagged. Surfaces at L10 (`surface.md`,
`interior.md`); belts at L08 (`belts.md`); landforms at
L11–L13; building stone at L13–L14.

## Igneous — cooled from melt

| Kind | Look | Forms | Ladder spots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basalt | dark, fine, flood plains | fast lava, ridges, floods | Moon maria, Deccan, Hawaii, Olympus flows |
| Granite | speckled light, coarse | slow continental magma | continental cores, Acasta's kin |
| Obsidian | black glass | instant-chilled rhyolite | Yellowstone cliffs, trade blades |
| Pumice | frothy, floats | gassy froth | ash falls, beaches after eruptions |
| Komatiite | spinifex blades | ultra-hot Archean lava | oldest greenstones only |

Sources: L10 `volcanism.md`; L11 `volcanoes.md`,
`continents.md:83` (granite/basalt densities).

## Dust, regolith, minerals — the grains

**Regolith**: broken blanket on airless worlds — Moon
grey, asteroid rubble, Mars dust; gardening by micromete-
orites, no soil without life. **Cosmic dust**: silicate
and carbon grains between stars (see L06 `ism.md:85-101`),
Stardust-sampled, iron-60 from nearby supernovae in ocean
crusts. **Minerals**: olivine and pyroxene (mantles),
quartz (continents), feldspar (highlands — anorthosite on
the Moon, L10 `moons.md:157`), zircon time capsules to
4.0 Ga (L11 `continents.md:76-79`), clays (playas,
`farmland.md:132`), gypsum plaster (L14 `shell.md:21-25`),
crystalline silica and the quartz-countertop hazard (L14
`storage.md:46-62`).

## Building stone — geology as architecture

Sandstone (Pottsville mass at Fallingwater, L14
`shell.md`), limestone facings, marble monuments, slate
roofs, clay tile, granite curbs, travertine lobbies,
brick from mud (`lifetime.md:37,49`), concrete from
limestone baked (`blocks.md:35,63-73`), glass from sand
curtain walls, steel from iron ore — every L13/L14
material with its quarry in this catalog's tables above.
