# Catalogs — kinds of things

This topic holds the cross-level catalogs: kinds of stars,
planets, rocks, and elements with content details per kind
— look, makeup, sizes, history, location on the ladder,
and real examples. Levels describe places; catalogs
describe populations. Every type is tagged **observed**,
**candidate** (likely but unconfirmed), or **theoretical**
(predicted, none seen).

Read order: this index first, then any catalog. Each
catalog links to the level pages holding the close-ups;
level pages link back here for the kind tables.

## Catalogs

- [stars.md](./stars.md) — star kinds: protostars, main
sequence O–M, brown dwarfs L/T/Y, giants and supergiants,
massive stars, variables, white dwarfs, neutron stars,
black holes, and the theoretical black dwarfs.
- [planets.md](./planets.md) — planet and moon kinds:
rocky worlds, super-Earths, sub-Neptunes, ocean worlds,
giants, odd cases (rogue, circumbinary), dwarf planets,
and moon types.
- [rocks.md](./rocks.md) — rock kinds: igneous,
sedimentary, metamorphic, meteorites, asteroid classes,
ices, dust/regolith/minerals, building stone.
- [elements.md](./elements.md) — chemical elements: where
each is made (Big Bang, stars, supernovae, mergers), how
common, where it shows up per level — gold, oxygen,
carbon, and the rest.

## How catalogs link to levels

- Stars: close-ups at L09 (`other-stars.md` future),
populations at L06 (`stars.md`, `dwarfs.md`,
`systems.md`), birthplaces at L05 (`clouds.md`).
- Planets: close-ups at L10, systems at L08, outer shells
at L07, neighborhoods at L06.
- Rocks: belts at L08 (`belts.md`), surfaces at L10
(`surface.md`, `interior.md`), landforms at L11–L13,
building stone at L13–L14.
- Elements: made at L01/L05/L06 (cosmic events), seen at
every level down to rooms.

anchor levels linked from each kind.
