# Element kinds — every atom's origin

This catalog details the chemical elements: where each is
made, how common, and where it shows up per level — gold,
oxygen, carbon, and the rest. All **observed** (spectra
and samples everywhere) unless a use is speculative. Made
at L01/L05/L06 cosmic events; seen at every level down to
rooms.

## The four forges

**Big Bang (L01):** hydrogen, helium, traces of lithium —
the universe's first three minutes made ~75% H, ~25% He,
and almost nothing else. Every star since burns this fuel.

**Stars (L05/L06/L09):** fusion to iron — carbon, oxygen,
neon, silicon, iron ash in cores and winds. Wolf-Rayet
winds seed carbon; AGB stars breathe out dust. Oxygen, the
third most abundant element, is star-made; carbon, the
fourth, likewise.

**Supernovae (L05):** past iron — the blast floods nuclei
with neutrons (rapid capture), building silver through
uranium in seconds. Iron K-alpha lines and ICM metallicity
(L03 `icm.md:24`) are these ashes, spread.

**Merging neutron stars (L06):** the heaviest — gold,
platinum, uranium in kilonovae. 2017's GW170817 glow
confirmed mergers as gold factories (LIGO). Titan's smog
organics and Earth's ores both end here in part.

## Abundance — the cosmic ranking

Hydrogen, helium, then oxygen, carbon, neon, nitrogen,
magnesium, silicon, iron — the top ten in the Sun, and
roughly everywhere gas has been measured. Rock follows:
oxygen bound to silicon (silicates), magnesium, iron
cores, aluminum crusts. Life follows carbon, hydrogen,
oxygen, nitrogen — the same top four, rearranged. Gold?
One part per billion of crust — siderophile (iron-loving),
sunk to the core at formation, delivered back by late
impacts and raised by geology into veins worth wars.

## Portraits the owner named

**Gold (Au):** made in mergers (and supernovae), sunk to
Earth's core, resurfaced in quartz veins and placer
gravels; worked since prehistory, still the standard that
outlives currencies. Ladder spots: L06 mergers, L08
belts (M-class metal), L11 ranges with veins, L13/L14 as
leaf, wire, and tooth.

**Oxygen (O):** made in massive stars; third most
abundant; breathed out of volcanoes as water and CO2,
then made free by microbes 2.4 Ga ago (see L10
`atmosphere.md`); rusts rocks, runs blood, greens aurorae
100–200 km up. Ladder spots: everywhere gas or rust is —
L05 clouds, L09 winds, L10 blanket, L14 lungs.

**Carbon (C):** made in stars (triple-alpha in giants);
fourth most abundant; graphite to diamond to fullerene;
backbone of life and steel, smog and soil. Ladder spots:
L05 dust and PAHs (L03 `groups.md:201`), Titan's methane
and smog, coal and limestone beds (L11 `plains.md`,
L13 stone), pencils and diamonds at L14.

## More portraits

**Iron (Fe):** fusion's ash and endpoint; cores of Earth,
Mercury, and asteroids; blood's hemoglobin; steel's base.
**Silicon (Si):** crusts, chips, glass — sand to
semiconductor at L13/L14. **Aluminum:** clays to cans to
airframes. **Uranium/thorium:** r-process heavies heating
Earth's interior (see L10 `interior.md`: birth heat plus
decay). **Lithium:** Big Bang leftover plus cosmic-ray
spallation; batteries at L13/L14. **Phosphorus:** found in
Enceladus spray (2023) — oceans, bones, DNA, fertilizer.
**Nitrogen:** Titan's and Earth's airs, ammonia seas
below. **Sulfur:** Io's frost, Venus's acid, gunpowder
and proteins.

## Where elements show per level

L01–L02: H/He gas, O VI–VIII ions (L01 `cosmic-web.md:127`),
dust and PAHs. L03: iron lines, ICM enrichment, molecular
H2 (L03 `members.md:103`). L04: gas phases cold/warm/hot
(`halos.md:260-284`). L05–L06: silicate/carbon grains,
iron-60, CAIs and chondrites. L07–L08: water/ammonia/
methane ices, C/S/M classes. L09: fusion chains, neutrinos.
L10: granite/basalt split, anorthosite highlands, zircon
dates, salt flats, flood basalts. L11: ore veins, coal,
limestone. L12–L14: quarries, countertops (silica hazard),
plaster, steel, glass, gold leaf.

## Uses — atoms at work

Silicon chips, copper wire, lithium cells, iron steel,
aluminum airframes, uranium fuel and clocks, gold contacts
and reserves, phosphorus fertilizer, nitrogen explosives
and fertilizer, carbon everything organic — the periodic
table as a hardware catalog for L13/L14, with quarries and
mines at L12 (`extraction-energy.md`) as the loading dock.
