# Planet and moon kinds — every world detailed

This catalog details planet and moon kinds: look, makeup,
sizes, history, ladder location, examples. Tags:
**observed**, **candidate**, **theoretical**. Close-ups at
L10; systems at L08; shells at L07; neighborhoods at L06;
exoplanet census at L06 (`systems.md`) and L09
(`companions.md:79-86`).

## Iron-rich worlds — cannonballs [observed]

**Look:** grey cratered metal-rich plains, weak fields,
long cliffs as the planet shrinks. **Makeup:** giant iron
cores under thin rock (Mercury's core fills most of it).
**Sizes:** sub-Earth. **History:** stripped mantles —
giant impacts or close-Sun evaporation. **Location:** L08
`terrestrials.md`, L10 `interior.md`. **Examples:**
Mercury.

## Earth-like worlds — the standard [observed: one]

**Look:** blue oceans, brown-green lands, white ice and
clouds, night lights. **Makeup:** nitrogen-oxygen air,
open water, granite continents, dynamo shield (see L10).
**Sizes:** ~1 Earth. **History:** outgassing plus
delivery, plates, oxidation, life (see L10
`lifetime.md`). **Location:** L10 all pages. **Examples:**
Earth. True twins sought; candidates exist, none
confirmed.

## Desert worlds — the dry middle [observed]

**Look:** tan rock, pink dust skies, dry valleys, polar
caps of ice and dry ice. **Makeup:** thin CO2 air, no
shield, water frozen underground. **Sizes:** sub-Earth to
Earth. **History:** lost blankets after dynamos died (see
L10 `atmospheres.md`). **Location:** L08 `terrestrials.md`,
L10 examples. **Examples:** Mars.

## Lava worlds — the molten [observed]

**Look:** glowing daysides, silicate vapor skies, magma
seas. **Makeup:** rock boiled by starlight on locked
daysides. **Sizes:** Earth to super-Earth. **History:**
migrated or formed close-in; tidally locked. **Location:**
L09 `companions.md`, L06 `systems.md`. **Examples:** 55
Cancri e, CoRoT-7b. (NASA Exoplanet Archive.)

## Ice worlds — the frozen [observed]

**Look:** bright ice shells, cracks and lines, geysers at
the poles. **Makeup:** water ice over salty oceans (see
L10 `hydrosphere.md`). **Sizes:** Moon to sub-Earth.
**History:** tidal or radiogenic heat under ice; snowball
Earths show the phase is reversible. **Location:** L10
`moons.md`, `surface.md`. **Examples:** Europa, Enceladus,
snowball-Earth eras.

## Carbon worlds — the diamond theoretical [theoretical]

**Look:** predicted: graphite crusts, diamond mantles,
tar seas. **Makeup:** carbon exceeding oxygen inverts
Earth's chemistry — carbides, not silicates. **History:**
forms around carbon-rich stars. **Location:** nowhere
confirmed; 55 Cancri e once suspected, now read as lava.
Listed as the test case for chemistry-driven kinds.

## Super-Earths and sub-Neptunes — the in-between [observed]

**Look:** unknown surfaces under thick airs or none;
the most common planets found. **Makeup:** rock with
percent-level hydrogen (sub-Neptunes) to bare rock
(super-Earths); the radius valley near 1.8 Earths divides
them. **Sizes:** 1–4 Earths. **History:** photoevaporation
or formation poor in gas. **Location:** L06 `systems.md`,
L09 `companions.md`. **Examples:** most Kepler finds;
Barnard's four planets (L06).

## Ocean worlds — all water [candidate]

**Look:** predicted: endless sea under steam skies, no
land. **Makeup:** percent-level water by mass, high-
pressure ice seafloors. **Sizes:** super-Earth to
sub-Neptune. **History:** formed past the snow line,
migrated in. **Location:** L09 `companions.md`, L10
`hydrosphere.md`. **Examples:** K2-18 b (candidate
signals debated), TOI-270 d (candidate).

## Gas giants — the deep airs [observed]

**Look:** banded ammonia clouds, centuries-long storms
(see L10 `clouds-weather.md`). **Makeup:** hydrogen-helium
with no surface; jets kilometers deep. **Sizes:** Saturn
to 13 Jupiters. **History:** cores that grew fast enough
to gulp nebula gas (see L08 `giants.md`, `lifetime.md`).
**Location:** L07/L08 `giants.md`. **Examples:** Jupiter,
Saturn, Proxima c (candidate cold giant).

## Ice giants — the blue middles [observed]

**Look:** methane-blue featureless disks, supersonic
winds. **Makeup:** water-ammonia mantles over rock cores,
thin hydrogen envelopes. **Sizes:** Neptune/Uranus class.
**History:** cores that grew too slowly for full gas
capture. **Location:** L07/L08 `giants.md`. **Examples:**
Uranus, Neptune.

## Hot Jupiters and puffies — the extremes [observed]

**Look:** inflated Daysides, escaping tails, densities
below cork. **Makeup:** gas giants roasted close-in
(hot Jupiters) or anomalously light (super-puffs).
**History:** migrated inward; puffies maybe ringed or
hazy. **Location:** L09 `companions.md`. **Examples:** 51
Pegasi b (first), Kepler-51 super-puffs.

## Rogue planets — the starless [observed]

**Look:** dark wanderers, glowing faintly in infrared
from birth heat. **Makeup:** like any planet, minus
sunlight. **History:** ejected from systems or formed
alone; microlensing counts them in the billions galaxy-
wide. **Location:** L06 `dwarfs.md:96-104`, L05
`open.md` JuMBOs. **Examples:** PSO J318.5-22, JuMBO
pairs.

## Circumbinary worlds — two sunsets [observed]

**Look:** skies with two suns, seasons from two orbits.
**Makeup:** ordinary planets in extraordinary orbits.
**History:** formed in circumbinary disks, surviving the
binary's tides. **Location:** L06 `systems.md`, L09
`companions.md`. **Examples:** Kepler-16b, Tatooine's
real cousins by the dozen.

## Dwarf planets — the small round [observed]

**Look:** hearts of nitrogen ice, mountains of water
ice, ringed waists. **Makeup:** rock-ice mixes too small
to clear orbits. **Sizes:** Ceres to Eris. **History:**
leftover building blocks, some differentiated (see L07
`kuiper.md`, `scattered.md`; L08 `belts.md`).
**Location:** orbits at L07, class here. **Examples:**
Pluto (heart), Eris, Haumea (rings), Makemake, Ceres,
Quaoar (rings past its Roche limit), Gonggong, Orcus.

## Moon kinds — the companions [observed]

| Kind | Look | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Volcanic | sulfur frost, fountains, 500-km plumes | Io |
| Ocean | cracked ice over hidden seas, plumes | Europa, Enceladus |
| Hazy | orange smog, methane rain, dune equator | Titan |
| Dead cratered | grey highlands, maria seas, rays | Moon, Callisto |
| Captured | dark irregular orbits, retrograde | Phobos, Triton (likely) |
| Ringed small | centaur rings | Chariklo (L07 future `centaurs.md`) |

**Location:** L10 `moons.md` canonical; L07/L08 giants
pages for systems.
