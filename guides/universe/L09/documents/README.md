# L09 — Stars

L09 = L9 (ladder row L9, Stars, ~10^9 m). This
folder is the L9 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #226): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l8.md` — L8 behind us: what the Planetary system
  looks like, what carries over into L9, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L8-to-L9 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `interior.md` — the engine: core, radiative and convective
  zones, energy transport, neutrinos, the dynamo seat.
- `surface.md` — the face: photosphere, granulation, sunspots,
  faculae, limb darkening, rotation.
- `atmosphere.md` — the crown: chromosphere, transition region,
  corona, prominences, coronal holes, spicules.
- `activity.md` — the voice: flares, coronal mass ejections,
  solar cycle, wind and Parker spiral, habitable-zone light, space
  weather.
- `companions.md` — the company: planets as nearby points,
  large moons, dust and sungrazers, rare stellar companions, preview
  toward L10.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: molecular-cloud
  collapse over tens of millions of years to protostar ignition and
  the ten-billion-year main sequence, red-giant swelling and helium
  burning, asymptotic pulses and nebula shedding, the white dwarf
  and far-future cooling, with a reading guide mapping each stage to
  its part doc and a cross-check of aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`system-in-order-nasa`,
`sun-closeup-sdo`), part close-ups (interior structure, surface
spots, coronal hole, flares, planets montage), timeline endpoint
(Helix Nebula).
