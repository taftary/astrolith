# LSB galaxies — the faint and the puzzling

This page is the L04 part doc for low-surface-brightness
and ultra-diffuse galaxies: Malin-type giants, Dragonfly
ghosts, and the dark-matter puzzles — the faint end dwarfs
and halos only ever footnoted. UDGs sit in L03
(`members.md:38-50`, Coma census); dwarfs in `dwarfs.md`;
streams in `halos.md:218-256`.

Most galaxy light is easy; LSB galaxies hide below the
night-sky glow — as big as the Milky Way with a hundredth
the stars, found by telescopes built to see faint (the
Dragonfly array). They split the field: some are failed
giants in massive halos, some puffed dwarfs, some may lack
dark matter entirely — each possibility rewriting a rule.

## The kinds

**Giant LSB disks** — Malin 1's record disk: enormous,
dim, gas-rich, barely forming stars — angular momentum so
high the gas never concentrates. Giants that never lit.

**Ultra-diffuse galaxies** — Coma's hundreds (Dragonfly
44 the massive one): Milky-Way-sized, star-poor, globular-
rich, dark-matter-heavy by most measures — failed giants
quenched early, or puffed dwarfs inflated by feedback and
tides.

**Dark-matter-deficient UDGs** — DF2 and DF4 in the NGC
1052 group: Milky-Way-wide, 1/200th the stars, at most
1/400th the expected dark matter (72 Mly by red-giant
yardsticks), oversized globulars — the first galaxies
found wanting glue. Formation unknown: tidal dwarfs grown
up (see `interacting.md`), stripped cores, or winds from
the giant neighbor's young hole. Debated (distance and
statistics contested) but doubled — a class, not a fluke;
19 more candidates wait.

Sources: NASA DF2 pages (2018, 2021); van Dokkum et al.
studies.

## Failed, puffed, or stripped

Three roads to faint: born diffuse (high-spin halos that
never concentrated), puffed up (feedback and tides
inflating dwarfs), stripped down (cluster harassment
removing stars and gas). Globular counts discriminate —
overrich systems like Dragonfly 44 kept massive halos;
DF2's oddballs suggest tidal birth. L03 keeps the Coma
census (`members.md`); this page holds the physics and the
puzzles.

## Key numbers

- LSB below sky glow; UDGs Milky-Way-sized, star-poor.
- DF2/DF4: 1/200 stars, ≤1/400 expected dark matter,
~72 Mly; debated, doubled.
- Dragonfly 44: massive halo, overrich globulars.
- Malin-type: record disks, gas-rich, unlit.
- L04 frame: 10^20–10^21 m — one ghost per view.

Sources: NASA DF2 pages; in-repo pages named above.

## Example objects

DF2 is the puzzle standard: see-through, glueless —
rules rewritten.

Dragonfly 44 is the massive standard: ghost with halo —
failed giant.

Malin 1 is the disk standard: record-sized, unlit —
spin too high.

Coma UDGs are the census standard: hundreds faint —
common ghosts.

Sources: pages named above.
