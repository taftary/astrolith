# Lenticulars — the armless disks

This page is the L04 part doc for S0 galaxies: disks
without arms — the bridge class spirals and ellipticals
only ever named. Mentions live in `spirals.md:25`,
`ellipticals.md:7,40-41,230,330`, and L03 `members.md:134`;
morphology context in `spirals.md:21-60` and
`ellipticals.md:22-43`.

A lenticular is a spiral that lost its star formation but
kept its disk: smooth lens-shaped glow, bright nucleus,
dust lane sometimes, arms never. Edge-on they read as
spindles with bulges; face-on as featureless disks with
rings. Clusters are full of them — the morphology-density
relation (see L03 `clusters.md`) peaks in S0s — because
clusters are where spirals go to be stripped and starved.

## Faded spirals versus mergers

Two roads make armless disks. Faded spirals: ram-pressure
stripping (see L03 `members.md` jellyfish) and strangling
remove gas; star formation gutters out over billions of
years; arms fade as massive blue stars die unreplaced —
fast rotators with disk kinematics intact. Merger-built:
minor mergers thicken and heat disks into lens shapes with
bigger bulges — slow rotators approaching ellipticals.
ATLAS-style surveys divide them by spin, not looks: fast
S0s are stripped spirals, slow ones are merger remnants on
the elliptical road (see `ellipticals.md`).

## Where they live

S0s love dense places: cluster cores run half lenticular,
the field runs few — the gradient that proves environment
makes them. The Spindle (NGC 5866) shows the edge-on type
with its dust lane; NGC 2787 a face-on ringed one; IC
1101's giant hides S0 structure inside a cD envelope (see
`ellipticals.md`). Dwarf S0s blur into dwarf ellipticals
(see `dwarfs.md`); polar-ring S0s blur into `interacting.md`.

## Key numbers

- Disks without arms; dust lanes sometimes; rings often.
- Fast rotators (stripped) versus slow (merged).
- Cluster cores ~half S0; field few.
- L04 frame: 10^20–10^21 m — one galaxy per view.

## Example objects

The Spindle is the edge-on standard: lens, bulge, dust
lane — no arms.

A cluster S0 is the stripped standard: fast disk, dead
arms — ram pressure's receipt.

A slow S0 is the merger standard: big bulge, hot disk —
elliptical road begun.
