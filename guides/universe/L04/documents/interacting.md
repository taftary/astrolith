# Interacting galaxies — collisions in progress

This page is the L04 part doc for interacting and merging
galaxies: pairs, tails, bridges, rings, shells, and tidal
dwarfs — the sequence spirals and ellipticals only ever
cited. Mentions live in `ellipticals.md:104-106` (NGC
1316), `:133-134` (shells), `dwarfs.md:128`, and
`spirals.md:193-195,371` (M51's tail); streams in
`halos.md:218-256`; compact groups in L03 (`groups.md`).

Galaxies collide constantly and stars never touch: the
spaces between stars dwarf the stars, so mergers rearrange
orbits, not surfaces — throwing tidal tails across
hundreds of thousands of light-years, ringing bullseyes,
piling shells, and birthing dwarf galaxies in the debris.
Each shape is a timestamp; read the sequence pair to
merger to remnant and collisions become clocks.

## The sequence

**Pairs** — bridges and first tails: M51's tail reaching
its companion (see `spirals.md:193-195`), Antennae
mid-embrace with twin tails sweeping 100+ kpc, the Mice
with clockwork tails. Star formation ignites where gas
compresses.

**Mergers** — chaos and starburst: Arp 220's dust-
buried blaze (see `starbursts.md`), NGC 1316's dust lanes
in an elliptical body (`ellipticals.md:104-106`), shells
layering around remnants like onion skins (`:133-134`).

**Remnants** — settling ellipticals: ripples fading over
billions of years, cores scouring binary black holes,
halos keeping the streams as fossils (see `halos.md`).

**Rings** — the bullseye special case: head-on passages
(Cartwheel) driving density waves into star-forming rings
with spokes, nuclei offset like yolks; polar rings circling
poles where a gas-rich dwarf fell perpendicular.

**Tidal dwarfs** — recycling: self-gravitating knots in
tails, dark-matter-poor by birth (no halo to inherit) —
the predicted kin of DF2-type puzzles (see `lsb.md`).

## Our future — the Milky Way–Andromeda merger

In ~4–5 billion years the pair meets (see `lifetime.md`):
first pass throwing tails, settling into one elliptical
with the Sun's descendants riding anonymous orbits —
possibly flung to the halo, possibly still bound. M31's
approach is measured; the choreography is certain, the
seating unknown.

## How it interacts with the others

Spirals supply (`spirals.md`): gas-rich disks making the
best tails and bursts. Ellipticals result
(`ellipticals.md`): merger roads, Sérsic profiles, red
sequence. Dwarfs participate (`dwarfs.md`): Magellanic
tides, Sagittarius dissolving, tidal dwarfs born. Nuclei
ignite (`nuclei.md`): mergers feeding quasars, binary
holes scouring cores. Halos remember (`halos.md`):
streams, Splash, Gaia-Enceladus as our own merger fossil.
Starbursts blaze (`starbursts.md`): ULIRGs as merger
peaks. LSB puzzles echo (`lsb.md`): tidal dwarfs versus
dark-matter-free mysteries. Compact groups rehearse (L03
`groups.md`): Hickson quartets mid-fall.

## Key numbers

- Tails to 100+ kpc; rings from head-on passages; shells
layering billions of years.
- Starbursts peak at merger; quasars ignite with delay.
- Milky Way–Andromeda in ~4–5 Gyr: one elliptical after.
- L04 frame: 10^20–10^21 m — one collision per view.
