# Entry — diving from L3 into Galaxies

This page describes the L3-to-L4 transition in
plain steps: what you see as you approach, the effects on entry, and what the
new level looks like once you are inside. The scale context (what L3 is, what
carries over) lives in [previous-l3.md](./previous-l3.md); what you meet inside lives in
[spirals](./spirals.md), [ellipticals](./ellipticals.md),
[dwarfs](./dwarfs.md), [nuclei](./nuclei.md) and [halos](./halos.md), dated in
[lifetime.md](./lifetime.md).

## The approach, in plain steps

1. **Pick the home portal.** Start in the L3 cell holding the Local
   Group. Thirty-two markers share the cell (cluster and group portals
   plus field populations); only portals open, populations stay points
   (ADR 0010). Target the Milky Way portal — ours.
2. **Watch the preview.** Above 0.02 rad the marker draws its interior
   inside itself at the exact positions the open cell will show, at true
   size (`marker + child x ratio`, ladder R7): first a smudge of light,
   then a tilted spiral coin — disk, brighter core — growing as the dot
   fades from full brightness toward a faint floor (0.15) at the open
   angle while its children brighten from 0 to 1, both curves continuous,
   so the moment of opening is a pure origin shift with no pop. At most 6
   markers (largest on screen) preview at once. Only portal markers
   preview: field populations are displayed and counted but never open
   and never preview, so the preview always resolves into galaxy
   geography — disks, bulges, companions — rather than a starfield.
3. **Fall through the milestones.** The L3–L4 span is crossed through
   two invisible magnification milestones, because its 2.17-decade span
   (ladder anchors e 23.15 down to e 20.98) exceeds the 1.5-decade
   single-crossing limit. Each is a silent exact no-op on the path (zero
   position, unit ratio): generation, snapshots, labels and previews never
   see them. The dive pushes them by proximity to the targeted portal and
   unwinds them the same way. You notice them only as continued smooth
   motion across just over two decades of scale (ladder gap note).
4. **Open at 0.14 rad.** Past the open angle (about a third of the view)
   the marker's dot eases to zero as the camera
   passes inside, and the marker you entered and the cell you are in are
   one object — its boundary sphere follows the same fade curve in the
   parent's era color; the open cell closes back into its marker below 0.10
   rad, re-targeted. You are now in L4, inside the Milky Way cell. The
   parent's siblings hang 1/ratio cells away (about 148 L4-cell widths for
   the 6.76e-3 ratio); nothing shallower is drawn.

## Effects and visuals on entry

**What appears.** Points become places: the smudge resolves into a
barred spiral — thin star-forming disk marbled with dark dust lanes
and glowing nebulae, a yellowish central bulge, faint halo around it
all (the halo's streams and ancient clusters have their story in
[halos](./halos.md)). Neighbor smudges gain shapes of their own: Andromeda's tilted
barred disk 2.5 million light-years off (152,000 light-years across, its
compact companions M32 and M110 beside it — Hubble's January 2025 mosaic
stacked 600 fields over 10 years to resolve 200 million of its stars),
Triangulum smaller beside it, the
Magellanic Clouds as ragged companions (see [dwarfs](./dwarfs.md)). What L3 showed as "member
galaxies" is now architecture you can point at — disks, arms and bulges
with the stories told in [spirals](./spirals.md) and
[ellipticals](./ellipticals.md), lit centers with the story told in
[nuclei](./nuclei.md).

**What fades.** The cluster context falls away: Virgo's 1,300–2,000
companions, Coma's thousands, the X-ray glow of the intracluster gas —
all shrink back into the L3 marker you came through. The stripping
headwind and the central cannibals stop being weather and become
somebody else's story — the [member swarms](../../L03/documents/members.md)
and the [hot intracluster medium](../../L03/documents/icm.md) you left
behind; here the story is the galaxy itself.

**What the markers do.** The L3 marker you entered closes behind you
into an ordinary portal, re-targeted, sitting 1/ratio cells away among
its siblings. Ahead, the L4 cell offers its own markers for the next
dive down to galactic structures: 32 in a sparse-group cell like ours,
up to 2,000 in a rich Virgo cell (ladder R6), taken up in the
[L4-to-L5 entry](../../L05/documents/entry.md).

Sources: ladder R6 (marker counts); the [L03 members](../../L03/documents/members.md) and [ICM](../../L03/documents/icm.md) pages
(what the weather was); NASA galaxy-type pages.

## Survey context

The islands you dive into are cataloged, not decoration. DESI's
five-year survey (completed April 2026, 47+ million galaxy and quasar
redshifts — more than all previous 3D surveys combined) pins each
galaxy's depth by its redshift, while Euclid (first data March 2025)
now images them by the billions — the same wall-and-void geography
[L02's entry](../../L02/documents/entry.md#survey-context) traces and
[L03's entry](../../L03/documents/entry.md#survey-context) weighs by
cluster. None of this changes the entry mechanics above; it is why the
preview's galaxy geography can be trusted as real structure. Sources:
DESI collaboration (`https://www.desi.lbl.gov`); L02 `entry.md`
(survey currency); L03 `entry.md` (cluster catalogs).

## How long it takes

The seeded autopilot runs L1 to L10 in about 40 seconds, through the
Local Group with Virgo as the rich sibling (ladder R8); travel time per
rung scales with its decade span (`Δe · ln 10 / k`), so the L3–L4 leg (2.17 decades,
cluster scale down to galaxy scale) is one of the shorter crossings —
the longest is the four-decade heliopause-to-Sun leg. That compression is
honest: rung gaps on screen stay proportional to the real decade gaps.
Sources: ladder R6 (navigation, travel time); R5 (anchors).

## Key numbers

- Preview starts: marker angular radius above 0.02 rad (at most 6 markers, largest on screen); dot fades full to 0.15 floor, children 0 to 1; past open angle dot eases to zero.
- Open: 0.14 rad; close back below 0.10 rad.
- L3 to L4 ratio: 6.76e-3; 32 markers per L3 cell; siblings ~148 L4-cell widths away.
- Span crossed: 2.17 decades (e 23.15 to e 20.98); L4 span 10^20–10^21 m.
- Invisible milestones L3–L4: two, silent exact no-ops.
- Autopilot L1 to L10: about 40 s total, through the Local Group with Virgo as the rich sibling.
- Andromeda: ~2.5 Mly away, falling toward us at ~110 km/s, 152,000-ly disk (Hubble 2025 mosaic: 600 fields, 200M stars); Milky Way band: our edge-on home view.
- DESI: 47M+ galaxy/quasar redshifts (five-year survey completed April 2026); Euclid imaging billions (first data March 2025).
