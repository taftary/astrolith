# Starbursts — galaxies on fire with birth

This page is the L04 part doc for starburst galaxies,
ULIRGs, and superwinds: the blaze phases spirals, dwarfs,
and mergers only ever cited. Mentions live in
`dwarfs.md:34` (BCDs), `ellipticals.md:69` (submm
starbursts), and `spirals.md` (Kennicutt–Schmidt law);
fountains in L05 (`disk.md`); winds in L10
(`clouds-weather.md` as analogy).

Most galaxies form stars sedately; starbursts do years of
work in a day — hundreds of solar masses yearly, whole
clusters igniting at once, winds blowing the galaxy apart
from inside. Merger or bar funnels gas to the center (see
`interacting.md`), pressure crosses the threshold, and the
galaxy lights up in infrared: dust drinking ultraviolet
and re-radiating heat. Then the wind ends it — superwinds
ejecting the fuel, quenching the burst into the red
sequence (see `ellipticals.md`).

## The gallery

**M82** — the superwind standard: edge-on starburst with
hydrogen filaments streaming thousands of light-years
along the minor axis — the outflow visible, the quenching
in progress.

**NGC 253** — the near dusty disk: Sculptor's starburst
behind dust lanes, super star clusters forming now.

**Arp 220** — the ULIRG standard: merger remnant glowing
a trillion suns in infrared, twin nuclei spiraling
together inside the dust — the local merger peak (see
`interacting.md`).

**Submillimeter galaxies** — the distant blaze: cosmic-
noon dusty giants (`ellipticals.md:69`), forming
thousands of Suns yearly, ancestors of today's giant
ellipticals.

**Blue compact dwarfs** — the small blaze (`dwarfs.md:34`):
dwarfs outshining their mass in bursts, local analogs of
first galaxies.

## Superwinds and fountains

Massive stars die fast and clustered: supernovae overlapping
into superbubbles that break out of the disk — chimneys
venting hot gas to the halo, some escaping, some raining
back as fountains (see L05 `disk.md`, `halos.md` gas).
Winds carry metals out and regulate growth: without
feedback, models make too many stars — superwinds are the
thermostat. M82's filaments are one outflow caught
mid-flight.

## How it interacts with the others

Spirals feed (`spirals.md`): bars and arms funneling gas
inward. Dwarfs flash (`dwarfs.md`): BCD bursts as local
dawns. Ellipticals inherit (`ellipticals.md`): quenched
burst remnants on the red sequence. Nuclei share fuel
(`nuclei.md`): starburst-AGN composites, winds combined.
Halos receive (`halos.md`): metals vented, fountains
raining. Mergers trigger (`interacting.md`): ULIRG peaks
at coalescence. Lenticulars remember (`lenticulars.md`):
stripped spirals that burst last. Lifetimes date
(`lifetime.md`): cosmic noon as the burst era.

## Key numbers

- Bursts: 100s of Suns yearly; ULIRGs to a trillion Suns
infrared.
- Winds: filaments kpc long; fuel ejected; quenching follows.
- Cosmic noon: the burst era; submm giants as ancestors.
- L04 frame: 10^20–10^21 m — one blaze per view.
