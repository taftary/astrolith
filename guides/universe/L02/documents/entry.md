# Entry — diving from L1 into the Cosmic web

This page describes the L1-to-L2 transition in
plain steps: what you see as you approach, the effects on entry, and what the
new level looks like once you are inside. The scale context (what L1 is, what
carries over) lives in `previous-l1.md`; what you meet inside lives in
`filaments.md`, `walls.md`, `voids.md` and `superclusters.md`, dated in
`lifetime.md`; the survey evidence behind the view is summarized in
[Survey context](#survey-context).

## The approach, in plain steps

You start with the whole Observable sphere in view and one portal targeted —
for our journey, the octant holding Laniakea, the home supercluster. Nothing
on screen changes as you move: entering or leaving a dimension is
deliberately uneventful at the frame level. What changes is inside the
targeted portal. Above an angular radius of 0.02 rad the marker draws its
interior at the exact positions the open cell will show, at true size — the
preview. At most 6 markers (largest on screen) preview at once. The marker's
dot fades from full brightness at 0.02 rad toward a faint floor (0.15) at
the open angle while its children brighten from 0 to 1, both curves
continuous, so the moment of opening is a pure origin shift with no pop.
Only portal markers preview: field populations (galaxies shown as points)
are displayed and counted but never open and never preview (ADR 0010), so
the preview always resolves into structured geography — complexes, threads,
sheets and dark gaps — rather than a starfield. Era color carries over
unchanged (L1–L3 share the early-era palette), so the preview also previews
the palette: no hue shift on entry.

Target visual (files in `images/`, credits and licenses at the bottom) —
what the preview resolves into: the observed web around us, galaxies colored
by redshift (blue nearest, green middle distances, red farthest), Milky Way
band across the middle:

The chart is built from the 2MASS Extended Source Catalog of more than 1.5

## Effects and visuals on entry

**The sphere becomes surroundings.** At the open angle (0.14 rad, about a
third of the view) the target opens: the camera passes inside and the marker
you entered and the cell you are in are one object — its boundary sphere
follows the same fade curve in the parent's era color, easing to zero past
the open angle as the camera passes inside. The Observable ball
does not shrink away; it stops being an object and starts being everywhere,
because you are now inside a piece of it. The parent's siblings hang
1/ratio cells away (about 68 L2-cell widths for the 1.48e-2 ratio);
nothing shallower is drawn.

**Two silent milestones.** The L1–L2 span crosses two invisible
magnification milestones, because its 1.83-decade span (ladder anchors
e 26.94 down to e 25.11) exceeds the 1.5-decade single-crossing limit.
Each is an exact no-op on the path (zero position,
unit ratio): generation, snapshots, labels and previews never see them. The
dive pushes them by proximity to the targeted portal and unwinds them the
same way. You notice them only as continued smooth motion across nearly two
decades of scale. Sources: ladder gap note.

**The web resolves in layers.** First the supercluster complexes brighten
out of the glow ([superclusters](./superclusters.md): Laniakea home —
about 500 million light-years across, ~100,000 galaxies streaming in one
flow basin (Tully et al., Nature 2014) — plus the Shapley pull), then
filaments thread between them ([filaments](./filaments.md): WHIM highways,
feeding nodes), then walls fill the gaps ([walls](./walls.md): CfA2, Sloan,
South Pole) and voids empty ([voids](./voids.md): Local push, Bootes,
Eridanus). What the 2MASS panorama shows nearby, the SDSS/BOSS wedge (~1.5
million galaxies to z ~ 0.7 plus 160,000 quasar forests at z ~ 2.2–3 over
10,000 square degrees) confirms in depth: the same wall-and-void topology
traced by the 150 Mpc baryon-acoustic ruler. Color follows era: L1–L3 share the early-era palette,
so entry carries no hue shock — the shock, if any, is structural: points
become bridges. Field populations (shown and counted, never opening) stay as
context while portal markers invite the next dive toward L3 cluster cells.
Sources: ladder R8 (10+4 form); ADR 0010; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (entry
points, preview lines); SDSS-III BOSS.

## Survey context

The web you dive into is the same one redshift surveys map in 3-D. SDSS and
BOSS proved the 150 Mpc baryon-acoustic ruler in galaxy clustering; DESI
sharpened it to ~0.24% overall (0.65% above redshift 2 from the Lyman-alpha
forest alone) across six tracer samples. DESI DR1 (public March 2025) holds
18.7 million high-quality redshifts — 13.1 million galaxies, 1.6 million
quasars, 4 million stars — and DR2 (first three years, March 2025 papers)
doubles the precision; the five-year survey completed in April 2026 with
50 million-plus redshifts. ESA Euclid (launched July 2023, first data March
2025 with 26 million galaxies) is mapping billions of galaxies to 10
billion light-years over a third of the sky — the next source of map
updates. None of this changes the entry mechanics above; it is why the
preview's wall-and-void geography can be trusted as real structure rather
than generated decoration.

Sources: DESI DR2 results guide (March 2025); SDSS-III BOSS; ESA Euclid
mission pages.

## How long it takes

The seeded autopilot runs L1 to L10 in about 40 seconds; travel time per
rung scales with its decade span (`Δe · ln 10 / k`), so the L1–L2 leg
(1.83 decades, 93 Gly down to Sloan-Great-Wall scale) is one of the shorter
crossings — the longest is the four-decade heliopause-to-Sun leg. That
compression is honest: rung gaps on screen stay proportional to the real
decade gaps. Sources: ladder R6 (navigation, travel time); R5 (anchors).

## Key numbers

- Preview starts: marker angular radius above 0.02 rad (at most 6 markers).
- Open: 0.14 rad; close back below 0.10 rad.
- L1 to L2 ratio: 1.48e-2; 8 octant portals per L1 cell; span 1.83 decades.
- Invisible milestones L1–L2: two, silent exact no-ops.
- Autopilot L1 to L10: about 40 s total.
- 2MASS chart: 1.5M+ galaxies to redshift ~0.1 (blue z < 0.01, green
  0.01–0.04, red 0.04–0.1), Aitoff projection.
- Survey depth behind the view: BOSS ~1.5M galaxies to z ~ 0.7; DESI DR1
  18.7M redshifts, DR2 BAO at 0.24%.

Sources: ladder R6/R7; 2MASS LSS (Jarrett 2004); SDSS-III BOSS; DESI DR2
results guide (March 2025).
