# IGM — the gas between, beyond the hot phase

This page is the L02 part doc for the intergalactic medium
beyond the warm-hot phase: the Lyman-alpha forest, quasar
sightlines, damped and metal absorbers, and the baryon
census — the structure WHIM detections assume. WHIM detail
lives in `filaments.md:56-70,146-159`; BAO-tracer roles in
`entry.md:81,94` and `lifetime.md:34,160`; FRB census in
`lifetime.md:93`; quasars as objects in L04 (`nuclei.md`).

Galaxies are the lights; the IGM is the room — most
baryons live outside galaxies down to redshift ~2, traced
not by glow but by shadow. Every quasar spectrum wears a
forest of absorption lines, each a cloud the light passed
through billions of years ago. Read the shadows and the
invisible structure maps itself: sheets, filaments, and
halos in gas, column by column.

## The Lyman-alpha forest

Neutral hydrogen drinks quasar light at 1215.67 Å; cosmic
expansion redshifts each cloud's bite to its own
wavelength — hundreds of lines per high-redshift spectrum,
the "forest" blueward of the quasar's own emission. The
cross-section is so large that even millionth-level neutral
fractions show: the forest is the most sensitive baryon
probe at any redshift, mapping density fluctuations
sheet-by-sheet through filament to halo as columns rise.
Metal lines (carbon, oxygen, magnesium, silicon) ride the
strong systems — enrichment already widespread. Modern
view: not discrete clouds in voids but fluctuating
Gunn-Peterson absorption from the cosmic web itself, lines
and trough differing by degree, not kind.

Sources: NED Rauch Lyα-forest review; NED Madau IGM
review; Carnegie Lyα primer.

## Damped and metal absorbers

Column density sorts the shadows: forest lines (diffuse
web), Lyman-limit systems (halo outskirts), damped
Lyman-alpha systems (galactic disks in absorption —
neutral reservoirs holding most neutral gas at high
redshift, the fuel gauge of galaxy formation). Metals tag
origins: alpha elements from supernovae, iron-peak later —
each absorber a chemical fingerprint of feedback (see L03
`icm.md` enrichment, L05 `disk.md` fountains).

## Quasars as backlights (and tracers)

Quasars serve twice: absorption screens for foreground gas
and structure tracers themselves — DESI's 2025 analyses
extract BAO to 0.65% above redshift 2 from the forest
alone (see L01 `background.md`, `lifetime.md`), when the
universe was ~3 Gyr old. Large Quasar Groups sketch the
largest structures known; magnetic fields and filament
radio emission remain the open frontier — no "magnetic"
detections yet anywhere in L01/L02 (see L03 future
`radio.md`, L02 `filaments.md`).

## The baryon census — found, at last

Half the baryons were "missing" at low redshift — too hot
for the forest, too thin for X-rays — until WHIM
detections (see `filaments.md`) and fast-radio-burst
dispersion (see `lifetime.md:93`) closed the books: the
forest holds most at high redshift, the WHIM most today.
The IGM is the main baryon repository across cosmic time;
galaxies are tracers, not containers.

## Key numbers

- Lyα 1215.67 Å; 100s of lines per z>2 quasar; neutral
fractions to 10^-6 visible.
- DLA = galactic disks in absorption; metals = feedback
fingerprints.
- DESI forest BAO 0.65% above z=2 (~3 Gyr old universe).
- IGM holds most baryons to z~2; WHIM most today.
- L02 frame: 10^24–10^26 m — sightlines crossing the web.

Sources: NED reviews; in-repo pages named above.

## Example objects

A z>2 quasar spectrum is the forest standard: hundreds of
lines — shadows mapping structure.

A DLA is the fuel standard: disk in absorption — neutral
reservoir counted.

DESI's forest is the ruler standard: BAO at 0.65% above
z=2 — expansion measured in shadows.

FRBs are the counters standard: dispersion finding the
missing half — books closed.
