# Lifetime — dated stages of the L2 web

The full dated timeline of L2-scale
structure (tens to hundreds of megaparsecs: filaments, sheets, voids,
knots): from inflation seeds to the far-future fate. Sibling docs carry the
per-part stories ([filaments](./filaments.md), [walls](./walls.md),
[voids](./voids.md),
[superclusters](./superclusters.md); bridge in
[previous-l1](./previous-l1.md), entry in [entry](./entry.md).
Baseline is Planck 2018 flat Lambda-CDM throughout: age
13.787 +/- 0.020 Gyr, H0 = 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc,
budget ~4.9% ordinary / ~26.5% cold dark matter / ~68.5% dark energy.
For the universe-wide spine this timeline hangs off, see the L01 companion
[Lifetime](../../L01/documents/lifetime.md); for the survey evidence behind
the present-day web, see [Survey context](#survey-context).

## The stages

**Seeds from inflation (10^-36 to 10^-32 s).** Exponential expansion
stretches space by ~10^30 (at least e^60) and amplifies quantum flickers
into density ripples of one part in 100,000 — the seeds of every later web.
No L2 structures exist yet, only the seed power spectrum whose tilt
(n_s = 0.965) and amplitude Planck measured in the microwave background.
Reheating converts the driving energy into matter and light and the hot
plasma era begins. Source: ESA, Planck and
inflation; NASA Webb early universe (Cosmic Dawn 50 Myr–1 Gyr framing).

**First sound, then silence (~380,000 years, redshift ~1100).** The plasma
cools to ~3000 K, electrons and protons form neutral hydrogen, light
escapes as the microwave background, and the sound-wave pattern freezes —
including the preferred galaxy separation of ~150 Mpc (~500 million
light-years) still visible as baryon acoustic oscillations in galaxy pairs
today. DESI DR2 now measures that ruler to ~0.24% overall (0.65% above
redshift 2 from Lyman-alpha forest alone). L2 over- and under-densities are
still linear; dark-matter wells have waited out the radiation era and can
now grow after matter-radiation equality (~47–50 kyr). Sources: ESA, Planck
and the CMB; ESA Cosmic eras (recombination at ~3000 K); SDSS-III BOSS;
DESI DR2 results guide (March 2025).

**First lights (~100–400 Myr, Cosmic Dawn ~50 Myr–1 Gyr).** The first massive
stars (theory: 30–300 solar masses, few-Myr lives) end the Dark Ages; their
ultraviolet starts ionizing bubbles. Webb pushes the frontier to
JADES-GS-z14-0 at z = 14.32 and MoM-z14 at z = 14.44 (~280 Myr), with star
clusters seen at 460–600 Myr. Only the
densest knots inside future filaments light up; inter-filament L2 gas stays
cold and neutral; no warm-hot filament gas yet. Sources: NASA Webb early
universe (Cosmic Dawn range, first-stars mass range, MoM-z14 record);
Max Planck MPIA (end of reionization).

**Reionization (~200 Myr–1.1 Gyr, redshift ~20–6).** Ionized bubbles percolate
(midpoint z_re = 7.68, complete by z = 5.3, ~1.1 Gyr per the XQR-30 quasar
sample of 67 sightlines). Low-density L2 voids and sheets ionize
first along open channels while filaments stay partly self-shielded. Webb
UNCOVER finds ultra-faint dwarf galaxies produce ~4x the assumed UV and
likely drove the process. A
TNG-style census puts knot-plus-filament mass share at only ~8% at redshift
4. Source: Max Planck MPIA (Bosman, end of cosmic dawn, z = 5.3);
ESA Webb dwarf-galaxies release; ESA Cosmic eras (200 Myr–1 Gyr span).

**Cosmic noon (~2–3 Gyr, redshift ~3–2; broad peak 2–6 Gyr).** Star formation peaks —
about half of today's stellar mass forms in this window, ~10x the present
rate — with quasars and black-hole feedback at
maximum. Filament-fed accretion runs fastest: cold gas streams along
filaments into knots, shocks start heating filament gas (the warm-hot phase
grows from ~11% of baryons at redshift 2 toward ~40–50% today), and the filament
mass fraction climbs steeply from ~30% at redshift 2 toward ~40–50% now.
Protoclusters assemble into clusters into superclusters; Hyperion
(redshift 2.45, 60x60x150 Mpc) shows a supercluster mid-build at under 20%
of the present age. Source: ESA, Cosmic eras (cosmic noon);
[filaments](./filaments.md) (width and WHIM growth, Yang/Migkas);
[superclusters](./superclusters.md) (Hyperion).

**Slowdown and takeover (~8–9.8 Gyr).** Matter-dominated braking ends;
acceleration begins ~9 billion years in (~5 billion years ago, "nine
billion years after the universe began" per NASA), matter-dark
energy equality near redshift 0.3–0.4 (scale factor a ~ 0.6). Linear growth freezes: large
filaments stop thickening in comoving coordinates, structure growth slows,
voids expand faster and merge hierarchically while sheets drain into
filaments. No new large bound structures freeze in afterwards; the Solar
System forms alongside (~9.2 Gyr). Source: NASA, dark energy;
[walls](./walls.md) and [voids](./voids.md) (draining and merger growth).

**Today (13.8 Gyr, 13.787 +/- 0.020 Gyr Planck 2018).** The depleted web: the 150 Mpc ruler confirmed in BOSS
and DESI clustering (DR1: 18.7M redshifts — 13.1M galaxies, 1.6M quasars;
DR2 BAO 0.24% overall, 0.65% above redshift 2);
knot-plus-filament mass share ~33% at redshift 0
against ~8% at redshift 4 (finder-dependent — NEXUS+ gives filaments ~46%
in ~7% of volume, MMF ~39% in ~9%); 40–50% of ordinary matter as 100,000–10,000,000 K
filament plasma (the warm-hot medium holding the "missing" baryons,
density 1–10 particles per m^3, confirmed by Nicastro et al. 2018 oxygen
absorption and Macquart et al. 2020 fast-radio-burst census), only
~20% in galaxies and clusters. Filaments are hot thin highways
(median width ~1.0–1.5 Mpc today, from ~0.3 Mpc at redshift 2); mergers are
rare; the gas is starved. The 2025 benchmark is a single pristine Shapley
filament (7.2 Mpc, 6.1-sigma excess, overdensity 30–40) with first X-ray
spectroscopy of pure WHIM in one thread. Cross-check against the part docs: the WHIM
interval matches [filaments](./filaments.md), the 8-to-33 share matches the growth story
in [walls](./walls.md), and the stalled assembly matches [superclusters](./superclusters.md) (Coma
unrelaxed, Shapley core still collapsing). Sources: SDSS-III BOSS; DESI DR2
results guide; Martizzi et al. 2019
(IllustrisTNG baryons); Davé et al. 2001; Wikipedia warm-hot medium;
Migkas et al. 2025 (Shapley WHIM); ESA "models were right" release.

**Far-future fate (~20–50 Gyr to 10^14 years, Stelliferous through Degenerate
eras).** Filament dissolution and
isolation: in comoving coordinates the skeleton sharpens, but filaments
thin and starve while clusters become isolated islands receding
exponentially in physical coordinates. Sequence: Milkdromeda merger in
4–8 Gyr (2025 revision: only ~50% chance in 10 Gyr); Local Group
coalescence by 10^11–10^12 yr; horizon cutoff ~150 Gyr (beyond-Local-Supercluster
light stops arriving); galaxy dimming ~800 Gyr; external galaxies
unobservable ~2 Tyr even in gamma rays; star formation ends 10^12–10^14 yr
as the last red dwarfs die to black dwarfs. The Local Group merges; Virgo grows
unreachable; no new L2 structures form; star formation ends near 10^14
years. The existing web stretches into disconnected bound knots, and
Laniakea — a flow basin, not a bound object — disperses. Sources:
Hoffman et al. 2007 (future of local large-scale structure); Wikipedia,
future of an expanding universe; Adams and Laughlin Five Ages;
[filaments](./filaments.md) (Laniakea dissolving) and
[superclusters](./superclusters.md) (transient superclusters).

**Heat death (toward 10^100 years, Black-Hole through Dark eras).** Continued expansion toward maximum
entropy: near-vacuum, no gradients, cold dark isolated remnants. Remnant
sorting at 10^19–10^20 yr (90–99% ejected, 1–10% into central holes);
proton decay 10^32–10^42 yr if GUT holds (else iron stars to 10^1500 yr);
Black-Hole Era 10^40–10^100 yr (stellar holes evaporate ~10^67 yr); Dark Era
beyond. The L2
concept dissolves — only event-horizon islands remain. Baseline assumes
constant Lambda (w = -1); DESI DR2 evolving-dark-energy hints (up to
~4.2 sigma with supernova combos, below 5-sigma discovery) keep the
branches in [Alternative fates](#alternative-fates) open. Source: Wikipedia,
ultimate fate of the universe; Wikipedia, future of an expanding universe.

## Alternative fates

Baseline above is Big Freeze / Heat Death (constant Lambda, w = -1). Each
branch below names its trigger and what it would mean for the L2 web; none
is confirmed. DESI DR1–DR2 (2024–2025) evolving-dark-energy hints keep these
branches open instead of closed.

| Scenario | L2 consequence | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Big Freeze (baseline) | Filaments thin into isolated bound knots; Dark Era past 10^100 yr | Constant Lambda holds |
| Big Rip | Horizon shrinks past clusters, then galaxies, then atoms — web torn in sequence; earliest end ~22 Gyr hence for w = -1.5 | Phantom dark energy (w < -1) growing with expansion |
| Big Crunch | Expansion halts, reverses, web re-collapses to hot dense state | Closed universe or dark energy turning attractive |
| Big Bounce (cyclic) | Crunch followed by new expansion and a new web | Loop/ekpyrotic variants; no observable support |
| Big Slurp (vacuum decay) | Light-speed bubble rewrites physics everywhere in 20–30 Gyr, or anytime | Metastable Higgs vacuum tunneling |
| No-proton-decay | Iron stars to 10^1500 yr, then holes, then dark era — longer tail | Stable protons |

Sources: ultimate fate of the universe; future of an expanding universe;
Big Rip; DESI DR2 extended-dark-energy analysis (w0wa fits, low-redshift
z < 0.3 signal).

## Survey context

The web this timeline tracks is the one redshift surveys map in 3-D.
SDSS/BOSS proved the 150 Mpc ruler in galaxy clustering (~1.5M galaxies to
z ~ 0.7 plus 160,000 quasar forests at z ~ 2.2–3 over 10,000 sq deg); DESI
sharpened it to ~0.24% overall across six tracer samples. DESI DR1 (public
March 2025) holds 18.7M high-quality redshifts and DR2 (first three years,
March 2025 papers) doubles the precision; the five-year survey completed in
April 2026 with 50M-plus redshifts. ESA Euclid (launched July 2023, first
data March 2025 with 26M galaxies) maps billions of galaxies to 10 Gly over
a third of the sky, with Q1 filament-connectivity at 0.5 < z < 0.9; DESI EDR
ASTRA (2026) classifies every object across ~175 sq deg as void, sheet,
filament or knot. Full survey detail lives in [entry](./entry.md)
(Survey context); the part docs carry per-structure detections in
[filaments](./filaments.md), [walls](./walls.md), [voids](./voids.md) and
[superclusters](./superclusters.md).

## Key numbers

- Inflation: 10^-36–10^-32 s, stretch ~10^30 (at least e^60), seeds ~10^-5 (n_s = 0.965).
- Recombination: ~380,000 years, redshift ~1100, ~3000 K; BAO ruler ~150 Mpc (DESI DR2: 0.24% overall, 0.65% above z = 2).
- First stars: 100–400 Myr (MoM-z14 z = 14.44, ~280 Myr); reionization 200 Myr–1.1 Gyr (midpoint z_re = 7.68, complete z = 5.3).
- Cosmic noon: 2–3 Gyr peak (broad 2–6 Gyr, ~10x present SFR); dark-energy takeover ~9 Gyr in (redshift ~0.3–0.4, a ~ 0.6).
- Today: 13.787 +/- 0.020 Gyr; knots+filaments ~33% of mass (from ~8% at
  redshift 4; NEXUS+ filaments ~46% in ~7% of volume); warm-hot medium 40–50% of baryons (10^5–10^7 K).
- Fate: Local Group coalescence 10^11–10^12 yr; horizon cutoff ~150 Gyr;
  star formation ends 10^12–10^14 yr; heat death toward 10^100 yr.

Sources: Planck Collaboration 2018 (arXiv 1807.06209); papers and pages named above.

## Caveats (alternates on the record)

- Age: 13.787 (Planck 2018) vs 13.772 (WMAP9) — dataset combos; local
  H0 ~70–73 tension shifts age priors by ~1% and exceeds 4–5 sigma.
- Recombination: 370 kyr tabular vs 375 WMAP vs 380 ESA vs 372 Planck peak —
  start vs peak vs complete definitions; z 1089–1100 standard, 1270 helium tail.
- Inflation factor 10^26 vs 10^30 — e-folds convention; ESA Planck quotes ~10^30.
- BAO ruler 147–150 Mpc — Planck vs SDSS calibration; DESI DR2 reaches 0.24% overall.
- Web fractions vary factors of 2–5 by finder — always cite method (NEXUS+ vs MMF vs DisPerSE vs Bisous).
- Filament widths 0.5–8.4 Mpc across studies — cite finder, tracer, boundary.
- Largest structure: Quipu ~400–428 Mpc confirmed vs Hercules-Corona
  Borealis ~3 Gpc disputed — incomparable tracers.
- Far-future dates are order-of-magnitude (Adams-Laughlin), assume constant
  Lambda and often proton decay; DESI evolving-dark-energy hints (robust at
  z < 0.3, still < 5 sigma) could revise them.
- Milkdromeda merger now ~50% in 10 Gyr (2025 Gaia+HST revision), not certain.
