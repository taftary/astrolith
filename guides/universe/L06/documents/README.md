# L06 — Stellar neighborhood

L06 = L6 (ladder row L6, Stellar neighborhood, 10^16–10^17 m). This
folder is the L6 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #223): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l5.md` — L5 behind us: what Galactic structures look
  like, what carries over into L6, what changes at this zoom, key
  numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L5-to-L6 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `stars.md` — nearby single stars: spectral sequence, M-dwarf
  majority, Sun home with Barnard's Star motion reference.
- `multiples.md` — binary and multiple systems: bound orbits,
  mass-transfer fates, Alpha Centauri triple home in one frame.
- `dwarfs.md` — brown dwarfs and white dwarfs: failed stars and
  embers, Luhman 16 weather, WISE cold record, Sirius B.
- `ism.md` — local interstellar medium: Local Bubble cavity, Local
  Fluff wisp, heliosphere with the IBEX ribbon.
- `systems.md` — planetary systems and debris: Proxima b, Barnard's
  four, Epsilon Eridani belts, Sun preview toward L7.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: cloud assembly over
  millions of years to star birth and mass-dependent aging,
  brown-dwarf cooling and white-dwarf fading, close passages and
  the Sun's path through local clouds, plus the far-future fate,
  with a reading guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a
  cross-check of aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`alpha-centauri-ab-hubble`,
`sirius-ab-heic0516a`), part close-ups (Barnard motion field,
Alpha Centauri wide triple, Luhman 16B, IBEX ribbon, Proxima b),
timeline endpoint (Sirius B size comparison).
