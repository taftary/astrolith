# L06 — Stellar neighborhood

L06 = L6 (ladder row L6, Stellar neighborhood, 10^16–10^17 m). This
folder is the L6 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #223): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Scope and scale

L06 covers one cell of nearby suns: single stars, bound multiples,
brown dwarfs and white dwarfs, the thin local medium, and the
planets and debris they hold, in the ~1–10-light-year range
(10^16–10^17 m), anchored on the Alpha Centauri distance at 4.37
light-years (4.13 x 10^16 m). It sits between the [L05 — Galactic
structures](../../L05/documents/README.md) above and the [L07 —
Outer solar system](../../L07/documents/README.md) below, under the
[Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder. Frozen scale
rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder holds the
descriptive reference only.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l5.md](./previous-l5.md)) first, then
the entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below,
then the dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind
each claim.

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only
the navigation-level numbers: L6 span 10^16–10^17 m; anchor Alpha
Centauri distance 4.37 light-years (4.13 x 10^16 m); Sirius 8.6
light-years out with its weighed white-dwarf companion; RECONS
10-parsec census 462 objects in 317 systems with M dwarfs 284 of
378 stars (~75%); Local Bubble ~1,000 light-years across blown by
~15 supernovae from ~14 million years ago, Sun inside ~5 million
years; Local Interstellar Cloud ~30-light-year wisp at ~7,000 K;
L5-to-L6 ratio 1.35e-2 with 32 markers per L5 cell (1.87 decades,
e 18.49 to e 16.62), L6-to-L7 ratio 3.55e-1 with at most 6 markers
(usually one) — see [key numbers](./previous-l5.md#key-numbers),
[entry numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the [dated
timeline](./lifetime.md).

## How this level differs from L05

[L05](../../L05/documents/README.md) shows the building site — one
glowing cloud complex among spiral-arm ridges, dust lanes, and
scattered clusters. Here one nursery resolves into neighbors: the
cloud that looked like terrain opens into counted suns — single
stars, bound doubles and triples, cooling brown dwarfs and fading
white-dwarf embers — drifting through thin local gas. The
star-forming weather (collapse, feedback, dispersal) stops being the
story and becomes somebody else's story; here the story is the
hearths themselves, steady for billions of years, with the thin
Local Bubble cavity and its Fluff wisp as the air between them — so
new census, parallax, and Bubble-mapping results enter through the
part docs, not this index.
