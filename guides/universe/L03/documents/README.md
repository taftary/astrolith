# L03 — Galaxy clusters and groups

L03 = L3 (ladder row L3, Galaxy clusters and groups, ~10^23 m). This
folder is the L3 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #220): what its
parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through time,
and how they interact — each page with target-visual images and named
sources.

## Scope and scale

L03 covers one cell of cluster geography: rich clusters, poor groups,
the intracluster medium, and member galaxies in the few-Mpc range,
anchored on the Virgo Cluster (15 million light-years, 1.42 x 10^23 m).
It sits between the [L02 — Cosmic web](../../L02/documents/README.md)
above and [L04 — Galaxies](../../L04/documents/README.md) below, under
the [Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder. Frozen scale
rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder holds the
descriptive reference only.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l2.md](./previous-l2.md)) first, then the
entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below, then the
dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind each claim.

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only the
navigation-level numbers: L3 span ~10^23 m; anchor Virgo Cluster 15 Mly
(1.42 x 10^23 m); rich clusters 1–5 Mpc across, 10^14–10^15 solar
masses; poor groups 1–2 Mpc across, ~10^13 solar masses; ICM at
10^7–10^8 K with ~85–90% dark matter, ~5–15% gas, ~1–2% stars;
L2-to-L3 ratio 1.10e-2 with 32 markers per L2 cell (1.96 decades,
e 25.11 to e 23.15) — see
[key numbers](./previous-l2.md#key-numbers),
[entry numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the
[dated timeline](./lifetime.md).

## How this level differs from L02

[L02](../../L02/documents/README.md) shows the foam — filaments, walls,
voids, and flow basins inside a single L2 cell. Here one filament knot
is the subject: the bound city inside, with member swarms, hot plasma,
and lensing scaffolding resolved. The web becomes surroundings — the
filament you came down stops being a thread and starts being
everywhere, because you are inside one of its knots. The map behind it
is surveyed, not simulated — Planck SZ, ACT SZ, and eROSITA X-ray
catalogs count the cities, DESI (five-year survey completed April 2026)
and Euclid (first data March 2025) keep redrawing the roads — so new
catalog releases enter through the part docs, not this index.
