# Mergers — cluster collisions

This page is the L03 part doc for cluster mergers: bow
shocks, cold fronts, sloshing, offsets, and splashback —
the scale where two clusters become one. Bullet bow shock
lives in L01 (`nodes.md:40-45`) and `lifetime.md:99`;
sloshing in `icm.md:84-86,156-159`; assembly in
`clusters.md`; lensing offsets in `lensing.md`; radio
weather in `radio.md`.

Clusters grow by eating: groups and subclusters falling in
at thousands of km/s, gas shocking, dark matter sailing
through, galaxies trailing behind. A merger is the largest
explosion since the Big Bang by energy — spread over
billions of years, glowing in X-ray, radio, and lensing
mass maps. Each feature is a timestamp of the collision.

## The physics of the crash

**Bow shocks** — supersonic gas piling: the Bullet's
famous bow, Mach ~3, flight at thousands of km/s through
X-ray edge marking the front. **Cold fronts** — dense cool
cores sloshing intact through hotter surroundings, sharp
contact edges colder inside than out: survivors surfing
the merger. **Sloshing spirals** — off-center passages
setting cores oscillating for billions of years, spiral
edges in Perseus and Virgo. **Offsets** — gas rammed to a
halt while dark matter and galaxies continue: Bullet's
pink-versus-blue separation proving dark matter by
mechanics. **Splashback** — the apocenter edge where
infalling matter turns around: the cluster's physical
boundary, beyond the old virial radius.

## The gallery

**Bullet (1E 0657-558)** — the proof: bow shock, offset
gas and mass, lensing map — dark matter separated in one
frame (see `lensing.md`).

**El Gordo** — the distant giant: most massive high-
redshift merger, double relics, lensing magnifier (see
`clusters.md`, `lensing.md`).

**Abell 520, Abell 2744 (Pandora), MACS J0717** — the
complex: multiple subclusters mid-crash, dark cores
debated, filaments feeding — mergers as ongoing
construction, not events.

**Perseus, Virgo** — the sloshers: spirals, ripples, and
bubbles in nearby cores (see `icm.md`, `entry.md`).
