# Radio sky — halos, relics, and magnetism

This page is the L03 part doc for cluster radio emission:
halos, relics, mini-halos, bridges, bubbles, and ICM
magnetism — the scale where whole clusters glow in radio.
Sentences live in `icm.md:175-177`, `clusters.md:81-82,116`,
and `lifetime.md:89,185`; jets in L04 (`jets.md`); filament
radio in L02 (future `igm.md`); node context in L01
(`nodes.md`).

Optical images show galaxies; X-rays show hot gas; radio
shows violence: electrons accelerated in merger shocks and
turbulence, spiraling in microgauss fields, glowing across
megaparsecs. A cluster's radio face is its weather map —
halos where turbulence churns, relics where shocks passed,
bubbles where jets pushed.

## Halos and relics

**Giant halos** — megaparsec glows centered on merging
clusters, tracing turbulence re-accelerating electrons:
present in colliding systems, absent in relaxed cool cores
— the on/off switch proving mergers power them.
**Relics (shocks)** — arc-edged sheets at the outskirts
where merger shocks compress fields and fire electrons:
the Toothbrush and Sausage archetypes, polarized, spectral
index steepening downstream like smoke aging.
**Mini-halos** — small glows around central BCGs in
relaxed clusters, fed by sloshing and AGN leakage.
**Bridges** — faint spans between close pairs (A399–A401
via LOFAR): pre-merger filaments lighting as systems
approach — the cosmic web's radio debut, continued in L02
`igm.md`.

## Bubbles, cavities, and magnetism

Jets inflate lobes that displace X-ray gas — cavities as
negative images of radio bubbles (see L04 `jets.md`,
`icm.md`): Perseus's ripples, Virgo's stirring by M87,
ghost cavities from dead outbursts rising buoyantly.
Fields thread everything: microgauss strengths from
rotation measures, tangled on kiloparsec scales, amplified
by sloshing and mergers — the ICM is magnetized plasma,
and radio polarimetry maps what X-rays cannot.

## Key numbers

- Halos Mpc wide in mergers; absent in relaxed cores.
- Relics at shock fronts; mini-halos around BCGs;
bridges between pairs.
- Bubbles displace X-ray gas; fields microgauss, tangled.
- L03 frame: ~10^23 m — one radio cluster per view.
