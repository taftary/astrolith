# L08 — Planetary system

L08 = L8 (ladder row L8, Planetary system, 10^12–10^13 m). This
folder is the L8 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #225): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.

## Scope and scale

L08 covers one planetary system: the Sun with its ordered planets,
large moons, small-body belts, and the wind bubble out to the
heliopause anchor at about 120 AU (1.80 x 10^13 m, ladder e 13.25),
in the 10^12–10^13 m range. It sits between the [L07 — Outer solar
system](../../L07/documents/README.md) above and the [L09 —
Stars](../../L09/documents/README.md) below, under the
[Universe](../../documents/README.md#levels) ladder. Frozen scale
rules live in `docs/universes/ladder.md`; this folder holds the
descriptive reference only.

Read order: bridge ([previous-l7.md](./previous-l7.md)) first, then
the entry transition ([entry.md](./entry.md)), then any part below,
then the dated spine ([lifetime.md](./lifetime.md)) for dates behind
each claim.

## Figures at a glance

Canonical detail lives in the linked pages; this index carries only
the navigation-level numbers: L8 span 10^12–10^13 m; anchor
heliopause near 120 AU (1.80 x 10^13 m); Sun a 4.6-billion-year-old
G2V dwarf holding 99.8 percent of the system's mass, about 1.4
million km across; terrestrials Mercury through Mars (Earth at
1 AU with the Moon 384,400 km out); giants at Jupiter 5.2 AU,
Saturn ~9.5 AU, Uranus ~19 AU, Neptune ~30 AU; main asteroid belt
2.2–3.2 AU with Ceres at 940 km; Kuiper Belt 30–50 AU with Pluto
among its dots; termination shock 80–100 AU and heliopause near
120 AU (Voyager 1 at 94 AU in 2004 and 122 AU in 2012, Voyager 2
at 84 AU in 2007 and 119 AU in 2018); L7-to-L8 ratio 1.20e-3 with
32 markers per L7 cell and two zoom gaps, L8-to-L9 ratio 7.76e-5
with 32 markers per L8 cell and three zoom gaps — see [key
numbers](./previous-l7.md#key-numbers), [entry
numbers](./entry.md#key-numbers), and the [dated
timeline](./lifetime.md).

## How this level differs from L07

[L07](../../L07/documents/README.md) counts shells — the Oort
spheres, the Kuiper doughnut, the scattered strays, the comet
traffic with the boundary craft, all thousands of AU out. Here one
of those systems resolves into order: the counted shells fall back
into background while a single yellow dwarf takes center with its
four rocky worlds, its four giants as disks with rings and large
moons, its belt populations, and the wind bubble with the Voyager
edge — so new planet, moon, belt-census, and boundary results
enter through the part docs, not this index.
