# L11 — Regions of a planet

L11 = L11 (ladder row L11, Regions of a planet, 10^5–10^6 m). This
folder is the L11 visual and lifetime reference (Issue #228): what
its parts look like, how they were created, how they changed through
time, and how they interact — each page with target-visual images
and named sources. The bridge from the previous level and the entry
transition come first; the dated timeline runs through everything.
L11 opens the beyond-MVP tail: many regions per planet, then cities below.

## Bridge and entry

- `previous-l10.md` — L10 behind us: what the planet with moons
  looks like, what carries over into L11, what changes at this zoom,
  key numbers.
- `entry.md` — the L10-to-L11 dive in plain steps: approach, effects
  and visuals on entry, timing, key numbers.

## Parts

- `continents.md` — the base: landmasses, cratons, shields, growth
  and breakup, the bimodal Earth.
- `mountains.md` — the lift: ranges, highlands, belts, orogeny,
  wearing to hills.
- `plains.md` — the flat: plains, basins, lowlands, deserts,
  fill and drought.
- `oceans.md` — the blue: oceans, seas, coasts, islands at regional
  scale, tides and deltas.
- `rivers.md` — the flow: rivers, lakes, valleys, deltas, floods
  and droughts.

## Lifetime

- `lifetime.md` — the full dated timeline: crust and first continents
  over billions of years to supercontinent cycles and mountain building,
  ice ages and sea-level swings to today's regions, plus the far-future
  fate under continued drift and a brightening Sun, with a reading
  guide mapping each stage to its part doc and a cross-check of
  aligned timescales.

## Target visuals

`images/` holds the reference images, each with source, credit and
license in its doc. Bridge views (`earth-bluemarble-nasa`,
`region-himalayas-nasa`), part close-ups (global continents, ridge
valleys, farmland plains, brown bay waters, canyon lake), timeline
endpoint (ancient sheared rock).
