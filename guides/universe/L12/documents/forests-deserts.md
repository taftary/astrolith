# Forests and deserts — the green and the tan

This page is the L12 part doc for forests and deserts at
1–100 km: stands and groves, dune fields, playas, and
burn scars — the scale where one woodland or one erg
fills the view. The zones live in L11 (`biomes.md`); the
plains and basins in L11 (`plains.md`); city parks in
`landscapes.md` and the planned L13 green page; farms
cutting both in `farmland.md`.

From 10 km up, forests read dark green and textured,
deserts pale tan and striped — the two great land covers
at war by climate, and both managed, burned, and drilled
by people. A clearcut's geometry versus a fire scar's
ragged edge; a pivot's circle versus a dune's crescent:
from here, nature and management are distinguishable by
shape.

## Forests — stands, succession, fire

A forest at this zoom is stands, not trees: even-aged
blocks from old fires, plantations in rows, old growth
textured and multi-layered. Succession marches visibly —
lichen to shrubs to fir on glacial moraines
(`glaciers.md`), pine to oak on abandoned fields — and
forest types sort by water and winter (see L11
`biomes.md`): boreal crown-fire cycles, ponderosa surface
fires every decade, rainforest gap dynamics, mangrove
fringes in `coasts.md`.

Fire is the forester: stand-replacing crowns in boreal
country (see L11 `biomes.md`), frequent light burns in dry
pine, suppression building fuel until excluded fire
returns worse. Burn scars read dark red then green rebound
on yearly composites; salvage logging follows the roads;
megafire smoke veils continents. Bark beetles and drought
kill ahead of flames — grey stands, then torches. Towns in
the woods (`towns.md`) live with defensible space, ember
watches, and evacuation routes; the 2020s record seasons
wrote the current playbook.

Logging reads geometric: clearcuts squared to the road
grid, thinnings striped, plantations rowed — versus fire's
ragged wind-driven edges and insects' patchy grey. Old
growth survives in parks and roadless pockets, its
multi-century texture visible even from orbit.

## Deserts — dunes, playas, pavement

Desert at this zoom is wind's workshop. Transverse dunes
marching in parallel rows, barchan crescents migrating
downwind horn-first, star dunes piled where winds meet from
all sides — White Sands' gypsum white, Great Sand Dunes
piled against the Sangres, Saharan ergs striped for
hundreds of kilometers. Between dunes: gravel pavement
armored by wind, desert varnish darkening boulders,
alluvial fans aproning every mountain front, playas —
Badwater's salt crust dissolving in floods and
recrystallizing after storms (see L11 `plains.md:39-40`) —
and desert blooms after rare rains (NASA's 2026 Murchison
report).

Water decides everything: fog belts feeding Namib beetles,
washes flashing in storms, aquifers under dunes, pivots
greening circles where water is mined (`farmland.md`).
Overpumping drops water tables and the circles brown from
the edges — the High Plains story in miniature. Solar
farms and mines compete for the same flat sun (`extraction-
energy.md`): panels gridded where nothing grew, pits where
ore lay.

## How green and tan change over time

Forests migrate with climate and chainsaws: boreal marching
north (L11 `biomes.md`: +12% mapped), plantations replacing
diversity, beetles following warmth uphill. Deserts breathe
with rains: Sahara green 6,000 years back, Aralkum born in
decades (L11 `lakes.md`). Fire seasons lengthen with heat;
suppression debt comes due in single weeks. The far-future
in `lifetime.md` extends every trend: warmer, thirstier,
more flammable, with green where water is spent and tan
where it runs out.

## How it interacts with the others

Cities edge both (`cities.md`, `towns.md`): wildland-urban
interface, ember zones, dust on the sills. Roads cut both
(`networks.md`): logging spurs, fire lines, power corridors
sparking blazes. Farms replace both (`farmland.md`):
forest to field, desert to pivots — the survey grid over
green and tan alike. Water threads (`landscapes.md`, L11
`rivers.md`, `lakes.md`): washes, oases, crater lakes in
maars. Ice left the northern forests their ground
(`glaciers.md`): till, kettles, outwash under every stand.
Coasts fringe (`coasts.md`): dune woods, mangroves, scrub.
Volcanoes reset (`volcanoes.md`): blast zones to
wildflowers to fir in decades. Craters hide under
(`craters.md`): forests tracing Ries's buried rim. The sky
waters and burns (`sky.md`, L11 `weather.md`): rain,
lightning, smoke columns. Quakes strip slopes (L11
`earthquakes.md`): landslides opening light gaps.

## Key numbers

- Stands read by shape: plantations rowed, clearcuts
squared, fire ragged, beetles patchy.
- Boreal crowns in centuries; ponderosa surfaces in
decades; suppression debt due in weeks.
- Dunes migrate horn-first; playas dissolve and
recrystallize; fans apron every front.
- White Sands gypsum white; Badwater crust re-dissolving;
Murchison blooming 2026.
- Pivots brown from the edges as water tables fall.
- L12 frame: 1–100 km — one woodland or one erg with its
towns per view.

returning — floods remembered in crust.
